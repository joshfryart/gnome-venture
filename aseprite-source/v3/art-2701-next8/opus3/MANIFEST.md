# art-2701-next8, Opus third pass (opus3): richness

These are the same 8 icons as `../opus2/`, at 64x64 native. This pass builds on the opus2 scripts: the silhouettes stay the same, and it adds material texture, ramps, bounce light, small props and quirk detail. Every icon uses hard pixels and a 1 px ink `#2b1c26` outline, with no semi-transparent pixels and no pure black or white. `HP.assertClean` passes on all 8: the tags call it through `_o.lua` O.save, and the patch icons through `_p.lua` saveIcon. Nothing was copied into `game/`, and opus2 and haiku are unchanged.

Re-run: `/tmp/claude-0/ase/squashfs-root/AppRun -b --script-param out=<this dir>/ --script <this dir>/<name>.lua`
`_o.lua` and `_p.lua` are unchanged copies of the opus2 helpers.
