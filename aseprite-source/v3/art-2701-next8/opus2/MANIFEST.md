# art-2701-next8, Opus second pass (opus2)

8 icons, 64x64 native (same names as `../haiku/`). Hard pixels, 1 px ink `#2b1c26` outline, 0 semi-transparent, 0 pure black/white: `HP.assertClean` passes on every PNG (the patch icons call it from `_p.lua` saveIcon). Nothing copied into `game/`; Haiku files left untouched.

Re-run: `/tmp/claude-0/ase/squashfs-root/AppRun -b --script-param out=<this dir>/ --script <this dir>/<name>.lua`
Helpers: `_o.lua` = copy of `art-2701-opus/tags/_o.lua` (lighting, tubes, ovals, recentre, save). `_p.lua` = copy of `art-2701-opus/wear/_o.lua` (sewn-patch painter, icon I/O) plus an `HP.assertClean` call.

| icon | tag meaning | what changed vs Haiku | quirk | remaining weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hum | charm that also calms | Kept the two beamed notes but rebuilt them: lacquered jade notes with 6-tone light, tilted oval heads (each with one specular and a dark shade rim), slim 3 px stems with lit and shade columns, slanted beam with a lit bevel and dark underside.
