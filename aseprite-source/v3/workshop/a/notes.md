# Workshop A: "Same room, made rich"
- Idea: the same straight-on timber-and-plaster room and the same station spots (tap areas move a few px), but each station is rebuilt at hero scale with its own silhouette: stone hearth with a hanging copper pot, a jar hutch, a tool bench with vice and tool board, a potting bench, a big iron cauldron, a timber/stone/brick/rope pile, a floor loom.
- Light story: afternoon sun through the back window (soft beam in the air, bright patch with the mullion cross on the floor and rug); orange hearth glow on the slab and floor at left; a lantern halo; cool violet shade under the beam, under the benches and in the front floor corners.
- Depth: wall layer (window, peg rail, herbs, lantern, shelf, picture), stations, then a shaded barrel and sack in the front-left corner. Bottom-right 100 x 40 is plain floor.
- Quirks: gnome hat on the peg rail, tiny red door with a doormat in the skirting, pie cooling on the sill, cheese wedge with a bite out, sprout growing in an old boot, half-built birdhouse on the bench, a star twinkling in the violet flask.
- Ready layers (13, in layout.json): cooking tall flames (flicker) + steam (bob); pantry jar glints (twinkle); tools mallet (bob) + sparks (twinkle); garden budding sprout (sway) + can drips (bob); potions bubbles (bob) + bright brew (pulse); building dust puffs (bob) + glints (twinkle); decor shuttle (drift) + sparkles (twinkle). Nothing in the base is removed by them.
- Weak spots: the loom is low and wide (it has to stop above the nav-button zone), so it reads less boldly than the others; garden-to-loom air is only about 10 px.
- The tools mallet floats in mid-air (it is a layer, not held); the saw blade is small; the building pile's timber is mostly hidden behind stone and brick.
- Floor planks are fairly dark and stripy at the front; the beam in the air is a faint tint that crosses the pantry's right edge; lighting tints add many colours (hard pixels, but not a strict small palette).
