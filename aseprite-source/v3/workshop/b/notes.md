# Workshop mockup B: "Seven nooks"
- Idea: a gnome hall seen as a doll's-house cutaway. Upper gallery of four framed bays (pantry dresser, stone hearth, tool wall + bench, potting bench under a round-topped dusk window); ground floor of three bigger bays (cauldron on a tiled pad, building yard corner, loom + paint table). Carved posts, arched knee braces, a joisted gallery beam; each bay has its own back wall material so the crafts read apart.
- Light story: cosy dusk. One warm source per bay (three hanging lanterns upstairs, the hearth fire, the green brew + fire under the cauldron, two lanterns downstairs) with a stepped lamp pass in `workshop.lua`: warm near the lamp, violet-brown shade toward posts, beam and bay corners; warm pools on the floor under each ground bay. Flames, lantern glass, brew and the window sky are unlit "emissive" pixels.
- Quirks: gnome hat on a post peg; tiny green round-topped door in a post foot; crescent moon and star in the potting window; half-built birdhouse on the tool bench; half-painted toadstool ornament on the paint table; house plan (no letters) pinned in the building bay; mushroom and acorn carvings; bunting swags and drying herbs.
- Anim layers (`anim.lua`, 12 PNGs, listed in `layout.json`): cooking flames (flicker) + steam (bob); pantry jar glints (twinkle); tools mallet (bob) + chips (twinkle); garden flowering shoot (sway); potions bubbles (bob) + flaring brew (pulse); building new brick (bob) + stone chips (twinkle); decor shuttle (drift, no shuttle in the base so nothing is uncovered) + wet-paint glints (twinkle).
- Weak spots: it is a two-storey cutaway, so "back row / front row" became upper / lower row rather than depth on one floor. The light pass shows as stepped rings on the flat walls and floor, and the shade is on the gentle side. Upstairs stations fill their bays (5-10 px to the posts). The tools mallet layer floats over the saw; cloth bolts and the building stack are the least instantly readable shapes at phone size. Bottom-right 100 x 40 is plain floor boards with only a light pool.
