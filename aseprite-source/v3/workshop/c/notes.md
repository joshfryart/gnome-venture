# C: Garden barn, daylight

- Idea: a whitewashed barn-workshop that opens onto the garden. Back wall = a 4 x 3 pane sage-green glass wall plus an open barn door (sky, hills, beds, path, fence, kept pale). One-point perspective to (240,112): floor boards, ceiling rafters and side-wall boards all converge there.
- Stations: back row = brick bread oven with copper pans (cooking), duck-egg dresser of jars (pantry), potting bench in front of the glass (garden), bench + vice + pegboard (tools). Front row = copper still on a brazier with flask and bottles (potions), sawhorse + planks, stone blocks, rope coil, bricks (building), loom with rainbow cloth, yarn basket, paint tins (decor).
- Light story: morning sun comes through the glass and the door from back-right. It is projected onto the floor as stepped light panes (pale core, half-tone edge) fanning toward the viewer; unlit boards get a cool tint; cool violet shade under both benches; warm glow only at the oven mouth and brazier coals.
- Quirks: red gnome hat on a hook with boots below, a seedling growing through the floor boards, a loaf cooling on the oven ledge, veg basket and doormat at the door, hanging plant pots and herb bundles on the tie beam, a wheelbarrow outside.
- Anim layers (13): oven flames (flicker) + pot steam (bob); jar glints (twinkle); raised mallet (bob) + sparks (twinkle); tall budding shoot (sway) + can drops (twinkle); glowing flask (pulse) + bubbles (bob); teetering top plank (sway) + glints (twinkle); sliding shuttle (drift) + dipping brush (bob).
- Weak spots: the front row is smaller than the back row, so the floor between them is a bit empty; the floor light reads as diagonal streaks more than clear window panes; the vice is hard to read; the wheelbarrow outside is only a few pixels; the building "sway" plank moves under 1 px, its twinkle layer carries the signal.
- Building box is [150,200,272,262] (bricks widened in round 1); the oven chimney runs up to the tie beam, so the cooking box starts at y=10.
