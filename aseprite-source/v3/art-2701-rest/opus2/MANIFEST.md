# art-2701-rest / opus2: Opus second pass on the 8 Haiku icons

Not exported to game/. Same names and sizes as `../haiku/`: 7 trail map markers at 32x32 and `wayfarers-hat-b` at 64x64. Each icon has `<name>.lua`, `.aseprite` and `.png`. All 8 pass `HP.assertClean`: hard pixels, 0 semi-transparent pixels, no pure black or white. The trail markers have a 1 px ink `#2b1c26` outline from `c:finish`, with no non-ink pixel on the canvas edge. The hat keeps the base hat's own outline.

Helpers: `_trail.lua` is the Opus trail helper with `T.art` (32-wide rows, length-checked) and `T.pt` added. `_p.lua` is an unchanged copy of the sewn-patch painter from `art-2701-next8/opus3`.

Rebuild: `/tmp/claude-0/ase/squashfs-root/AppRun -b --script-param out=<this dir>/ --script <this dir>/<name>.lua`

I checked every icon at 1x and 6x on map green (`#6fa84a`) and on dark, next to the finished `art-2701-opus/trail` markers.
