# SA "The Rainroot Reaches": gnome wearables, concept round

Region: two worlds, cold bright highland (wool from woolly grazers, warp-striped weaving, felt, plant dyes like
cochineal red and indigo, quinoa, cold winds) and steaming rainforest (rubber-tree latex, palm fibre, bark cloth,
kapok fluff, giant leaves, macaws, morpho butterflies, tree frogs, leaf-cutter ants, cacao, passionfruit).
Each piece below is either highland, rainforest, or a crossing between them. No symbols, crests or costume pieces:
materials, plants, animals, weather and plain craft only.

Ramps are 6-tone dark -> light like `gnome-wear/_gw.lua` `W.R`. New ramps live in `common.lua` (`C.R`);
`W.R` ramps are reused by name.
