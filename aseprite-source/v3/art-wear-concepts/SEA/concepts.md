# SEA "The Monsoon Tangle" - wearable concepts (Hearth Pop v3)

Region: South-east Asia, stylised. Climate trait **Steamy**: everything needs water more often and grows a bit faster.
Sources drawn on: monsoon rain and paddy mud, mangrove flats, rainforest leaves (banana, nipa and pandan palm),
rattan and bamboo craft, wax-resist (batik) and tie-resist (ikat) dyeing, indigo, kapok fibre, clay water jars,
small forest animals (frogs, geckos, kingfishers, mudskippers, fireflies, fiddler crabs). No national, religious or
ceremonial symbols; motifs are plain dots, waves, zigzags and flowers.

Palettes are 6-tone ramps (dark -> light) defined in `common.lua` (`C.R`), plus the shared `_gw.lua` ramps.
Outline is always ink `#2b1c26`; light from upper left.
