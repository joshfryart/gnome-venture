# Olivewhistle Shore (SE) wearable concepts

Region: Southern Europe / the Mediterranean, stylised for a travelling gnome. Climate trait: long sunny days, fruit trees ripen faster.
Draw from: olive groves, lemon and fig trees, vines, cork oak, thyme and rosemary hillsides, goats and sheep, sardine boats, sailcloth and rope, esparto and wicker weaving, terracotta, saddle leather, linen, sun-faded stripes.
No flags, crests, saints, or costume caricature. Ramps are 6 tones, dark to light, like `W.R` in `gnome-wear/_gw.lua`; new ramps live in `common.lua`.

Shared ramps (common.lua): `olive`, `sage` (silver olive leaf), `lemon`, `terra`, `sea`, `linen`, `jute`, `cork`, `grape`, `wine`, `fleece`, `goat`, `indigo`, `coral`, `saffron`, `wicker`; reused from `W.R`: `leather`, `strap`, `felt`, `brass`, `wood`, `steel`.
