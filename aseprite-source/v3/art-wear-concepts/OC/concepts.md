# OC: The Coralscatter (Australia, New Zealand & the Pacific Islands) - wearable concepts

Climate trait: *Island time*: travel between zones is slower, finds are richer.
Design angle: things a travelling gnome wears for long, slow island-hopping and dusty outback walking. Sun, salt, sudden rain, reef and red dust. Materials: pandanus and flax weaving, coconut fibre (coir), bark cloth, sheepskin and merino, oilskin, kangaroo-style suede, carved mangrove wood, shell buttons. Motifs come from plants (gum leaves, wattle, hibiscus, frangipani, fern fronds), animals (gecko, possum, crab, turtle, seahorse, lizard) and weather. No flags, crests, national emblems or sacred patterns; bark-cloth and weave patterns are plain geometric stamps.

Ramps are 6 tones, dark to light, same style as `gnome-wear/_gw.lua` `W.R`. New ramps live in `common.lua` (`C.R`). Outline ink `#2b1c26`, light from upper left.
