# Maizehollow (CA) wearables: concepts

Region: Maizehollow (Mexico & Central America). Climate trait: dry spells, water lasts a little shorter.
Sources: maize fields, agave/maguey fibre (ixtle), palm weaving, backstrap-loom cotton, cochineal red and indigo dyes,
tooled leather, copper work, gourds and clay for carrying water, cloud-forest frogs, hummingbirds, monarchs, geckos.
Stylised gnome craft only: no flags, crests, religious or national symbols, no costume caricature.

Ramps (6 tones, dark to light, defined in `common.lua` beside the shared `W.R` ones):
`cochi` cochineal crimson, `indigo`, `maize` gold, `agave` blue-green, `straw` palm/ixtle fibre, `clay` terracotta,
`cotton` warm cream, `marig` marigold, `turq` turquoise, `cacao`, `copper`, `jade` leaf green, `bougain` magenta,
plus shared `leather`, `strap`, `wood`, `sole`, `brass`, `water`.
