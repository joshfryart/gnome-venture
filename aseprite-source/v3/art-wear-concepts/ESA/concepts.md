# Thornacre Savanna (ESA): wearable concepts

Region: East & Southern Africa, stylised for Gnome Venture. Climate trait: *Highland sun: grains and coffee grow faster.*
Sources of inspiration (materials, nature and plain craft only): acacia and baobab, sisal and raffia fibre, coiled baskets,
calabash gourds, beaten fig-bark cloth, tanned leather, brass and copper wire, striped and checked handloom cotton,
indigo resist dyeing, coffee and sorghum/teff in the highlands, banana groves in the rainy season, red dust in the dry
season, and the animals of the grassland (giraffe, zebra, guinea fowl, weaver birds, agama lizards, sunbirds).
Everything is a gnome's travel kit: small, practical, a little cosy. No flags, crests, religious or national symbols.

Ramps follow the `_gw.lua` style: six tones, dark to light, lit from the upper left. Ramp names with `W.` are the
existing shared ramps; the others are new to this folder (`common.lua`).
