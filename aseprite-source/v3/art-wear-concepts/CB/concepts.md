# CB "The Sunsprinkle Isles" (The Caribbean): wearable concepts

Climate trait: sea breeze, perennials regrow faster. The set leans on what the isles give a travelling gnome:
palm and pandanus plaiting, sisal rope, mahogany and calabash carving, sea-cotton, indigo and batik dyeing,
sailcloth, bamboo, shells and sea-glass, and the animals of reef, mangrove and garden (crabs, anoles,
tree frogs, hummingbirds, parrots, turtles). No flags, crests or costume pieces; plain craft, flora and fauna.

Palettes name the 6-tone ramps in `common.lua` (`C.R`, island ramps added on top of `W.R` from `gnome-wear/_gw.lua`).
