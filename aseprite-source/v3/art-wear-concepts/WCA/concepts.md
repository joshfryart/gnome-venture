# WCA "The Greatcanopy" - wearable concepts (Hearth Pop v3)

Region: West & Central Africa, stylised as a warm, wet canopy forest that opens onto savannah edges.
Climate trait: *Rainy season: water lasts longer, weeds grow faster.*
Craft and nature sources used (materials and plain craft ideas only; no symbols, crests or costume):
narrow-strip loom weaving, indigo resist dyeing (plain dots and rings), mud-dyed cloth (plain dots and
dashes), tooled and dyed leatherwork, raffia and palm-fibre plaiting, carved hardwood, calabash gourds,
cowrie shells, lost-wax brass beads, kapok cotton; plantain leaves, cocoa pods, hibiscus, oil-palm fruit;
tree frogs, snails, chameleons, hornbills, grey parrots, pangolins.

Ramps (6 tones, dark -> light, same style as `gnome-wear/_gw.lua` W.R; new ones live in `common.lua` C.R):
`indigo`, `ochre` (mud-dye earth), `raffia` (straw), `redleather`, `plantain` (bright leaf green),
`mahogany` (carved wood), `calabash` (gourd yellow-tan), `cotton` (kapok cream), `kente` (gold),
`forest` (deep green), `palm` (palm-oil orange), `mud` (dark mud-cloth brown), plus reused
`brass`, `strap`, `water`, `ember`, `steel` from `_gw.lua`.
