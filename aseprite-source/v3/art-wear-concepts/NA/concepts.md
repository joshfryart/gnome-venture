# The Maplewold (NA) - wearable concepts

Region: North America (Canada, USA). Climate trait: crisp winters (cool crops +10%, tropical crops -10%).
Sources of inspiration: maple and birch woods, pine forest, snow and frost, lakes and loons, beavers and
moose country, prairie wheat, bog cranberries, maple sugaring, canoe portages, plaid wool and quilted down,
waxed oilskin from the Atlantic coast. Plain materials and craft only: no flags, crests or borrowed
ceremonial/cultural dress.

Palette notes: every ramp is 6 tones dark -> light in the `_gw.lua` style; ramps reused from `W.R` are named
as such, new ones are defined in `common.lua` (`N.*`). Ink outline `#2b1c26` everywhere.
