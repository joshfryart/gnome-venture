# EA "Blossomreach" — wearable concepts (20)

Region: East Asia, stylised. Climate trait: *Four seasons: perennials give a little extra.*
Sources used: rice straw and hemp cord, bamboo and rattan, lacquered wood, indigo dyeing and running-stitch
mending, cotton quilting, silk, ramie, steppe felt and fleece, oiled paper, palm-fibre rain capes; plum and
cherry blossom, ginkgo, maple, persimmon, moss; frogs, cicadas, dragonflies, white-eyes, snails, fireflies.
No national, religious or heraldic signs: only materials, plants, animals, weather and plain craft.
The set is spread across the four seasons on purpose, so each category has a spring, summer, autumn and
winter piece (plus one wildcard), which is the region's trait made visible.

Palettes name 6-tone ramps (dark to light) in the `_gw.lua` style; new ramps are defined in `common.lua`.
