# Spicepeak (CSA) wearables: 20 concepts

Region: Central & South Asia, "Spicepeak". Climate trait: Monsoon (spices and herbs grow faster).
Sources of inspiration: high cold plateaus and wet monsoon lowlands; yak, goat and sheep wool,
pressed felt, ikat and block-print dyeing (indigo, madder, turmeric), running-stitch quilting,
leatherwork, hammered copper and brass, bamboo and palm-leaf weaving; spices (chilies, cardamom,
cinnamon), tea, pomegranates, parakeets, frogs, snails, geese. No religious, national or flag symbols.

Palettes are 6-tone ramps (dark -> light) in the `_gw.lua` style; new ramps live in `common.lua`
(`C.R.<name>`), existing ones are `W.R.<name>`. All icons: 64x64, 1 px ink outline `#2b1c26`,
light from upper left, transparent background, no ground shadow.

## 1. Footwear

1. **Curl-Toe Slippers** (`footwear-1-curl-toe-slippers`)
   Soft madder-red leather slip-ons with a long toe that curls up and back, gold running stitch on the vamp.
   Region: embroidered leather slippers with turned-up toes from the plains' cobblers.
   Palette: madder (red leather), brass (thread), lining (tan sole).
   Quirk: a tiny saffron-yellow tassel bobbing on the tip of the curl.
2. **Felted Snow Boots** (`footwear-2-felted-snow-boots`)
   Chunky undyed felt boots, seamless and thick, with a madder-red spiral appliqué on the shin.
   Region: pressed sheep-wool felt of the cold high steppe and mountain valleys.
   Palette: feltc (cream wool), madder (appliqué), sole.
   Quirk: a tuft of shaggy yak wool poking out of the top of the boot.
3. **Toe-Ring Sandals** (`footwear-3-toe-ring-sandals`)
   Flat tan leather sandals seen from above, braided toe ring and crossed straps, open for monsoon heat.
   Region: hand-tooled leather sandals of the hot, wet lowlands that dry fast after rain.
   Palette: leather, strap, brass (stud).
   Quirk: a small red chili hooked through the braided strap.
4. **Stilt Clogs** (`footwear-4-stilt-clogs`)
   Raised wooden clogs on two tall teeth with a woven jute strap, for stepping over monsoon puddles and mud.
   Region: monsoon streets and rice terraces flood; raised wooden soles keep toes dry.
   Palette: wood, bamboo (jute strap), water (puddle drip).
   Quirk: a little snail riding on the front tooth.
5. **Wrapped Hide Boots** (`footwear-5-wrapped-hide-boots`)
   Tall dark yak-hide boots with a woven striped leg-wrap wound up the shin and tied with a cord.
   Region: high-pass herders wrap their legs against snow and scree; striped woven bands.
   Palette: yak (hide), yarnB + saffron (stripes), strap (cord).
   Quirk: a saffron pom-pom on the end of the tie cord.

## 2. Backpack

1. **Chili Cone Basket** (`backpack-1-chili-cone-basket`)
   Tall conical woven bamboo basket carried on the back with a head strap, heaped with red chilies.
   Region: hill porters' cone baskets; the monsoon makes the spice harvest heavy.
   Palette: bamboo (weave), ember (chilies), strap (head band).
   Quirk: one chili tumbling off the top edge.
2. **Ikat Saddle Satchel** (`backpack-2-ikat-saddle-satchel`)
   Squarish woven satchel with blurred-edge ikat diamonds, a flap and tassels hanging from the corners.
   Region: resist-dyed ikat weaving of the Central Asian oases.
   Palette: indigo + saffron + madder (ikat), strap.
   Quirk: a pomegranate tucked under the flap.
3. **Porter's Frame Pack** (`backpack-3-porters-frame-pack`)
   A-shaped wooden carrying frame with a hammered copper pot and a rolled striped rug lashed on with rope.
   Region: mountain trail porters and copper-smith work of the valleys.
   Palette: wood (frame), copper (pot), madder/feltc (rug), canvas (rope).
   Quirk: a wooden ladle hooked on the pot's handle.
4. **Block-Print Bundle** (`backpack-4-block-print-bundle`)
   A big square of indigo block-printed cloth knotted into a bundle with two ear-like knot tips and a shoulder loop.
   Region: hand block printing with indigo; the cloth bundle is the simplest traveller's pack.
   Palette: indigo (cloth), feltc (printed dots).
   Quirk: a cinnamon stick poking out of the knot.
5. **Stacked Tin Pack** (`backpack-5-stacked-tin-pack`)
   Three stacked round brass tins clipped together with a carry handle, on leather straps: a travelling spice and lunch stack.
   Region: stacking metal food tins and brass-smithing; carrying spices on the road.
   Palette: brass (tins), copper (rims), strap.
   Quirk: a small steam curl rising from the lid vent.

## 3. Coat

1. **Ikat Robe Coat** (`coat-1-ikat-robe-coat`)
   Long quilted robe coat with wide sleeves and bold vertical ikat stripes, laid out flat.
   Region: padded, stripe-woven robe coats of the steppe oasis towns.
   Palette: madder + saffron + yarnB (stripes), lining (edge binding).
   Quirk: a little purple saffron-crocus flower tucked at the collar.
2. **Kantha Patch Vest** (`coat-2-kantha-patch-vest`)
   Sleeveless vest made of layered old cloth patches held together with rows of white running stitch.
   Region: thrifty running-stitch quilting from layers of worn cloth.
   Palette: plum, yarnB, saffron, madder (patches), feltc (stitches).
   Quirk: a big button made from half a walnut shell.
3. **Fleece-Collar Jacket** (`coat-3-fleece-collar-jacket`)
   Brown hide jacket with thick cream curly fleece at the collar, cuffs and hem.
   Region: sheepskin and yak hide jackets of the cold high plateau.
   Palette: yak (hide), feltc (fleece).
   Quirk: a turquoise bead toggle at the throat.
4. **Mirror-Work Smock** (`coat-4-mirror-work-smock`)
   Short pull-over smock in indigo with a madder yoke studded with small round mirrors in chain-stitch rings.
   Region: mirror embroidery of the desert-edge villages.
   Palette: indigo (body), madder (yoke), steel (mirrors), saffron (stitch).
   Quirk: one mirror catching a bright sparkle.
5. **Turmeric Wrap Coat** (`coat-5-turmeric-wrap-coat`)
   Light cotton crossover coat that wraps across the chest and ties at the side with long strings; turmeric dyed.
   Region: turmeric dye and airy wrap-front coats for monsoon heat.
   Palette: saffron (cloth), madder (ties, trim).
   Quirk: a fresh tea leaf pinned on the chest.

## 4. Cloak

1. **Palm-Leaf Rain Hood** (`cloak-1-palm-leaf-rain-hood`)
   A curved shell of overlapping dried palm leaves that covers head and back like a turtle shell.
   Region: leaf rain shells worn by field workers in monsoon downpours.
   Palette: palm (dried leaf), bamboo (binding), water (drops).
   Quirk: a little green frog sitting on top.
2. **Fine Wool Shawl** (`cloak-2-fine-wool-shawl`)
   Soft, fine goat-wool shawl folded in a draped triangle, woven border and a long fringe.
   Region: famously fine mountain goat wool shawls of the northern valleys.
   Palette: feltc (wool), madder + yarnB (border).
   Quirk: a tiny moth resting on the fold.
3. **Felt Shepherd Cape** (`cloak-3-felt-shepherd-cape`)
   A stiff charcoal felt cape with squared shoulders that stands on its own, spiral stitching down the front.
   Region: thick pressed-felt capes of the steppe shepherds; they shed rain and wind.
   Palette: charcoal (felt), feltc (stitching), brass (bell).
   Quirk: a small brass bell on the neck tie.
4. **Striped Yak Poncho** (`cloak-4-striped-yak-poncho`)
   Square woven poncho in bold multicolour bands with a neck slit and knotted fringe along the bottom.
   Region: striped hand-loomed wool aprons and blankets of the high Himalaya.
   Palette: madder, saffron, yarnB, indigo (bands), yak (neck binding).
   Quirk: a grey-and-black barred goose feather tucked into the neck.
5. **Indigo Dot Cloak** (`cloak-5-indigo-dot-cloak`)
   Hooded hanging cloak in indigo with resist-dyed dot rows, the hood ending in a soft point.
   Region: indigo vats and tie-resist dotted dyeing.
   Palette: indigo (cloth), feltc (dots), madder (lining).
   Quirk: a little green parakeet perched on the hood point.
