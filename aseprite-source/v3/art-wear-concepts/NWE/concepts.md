# Mistmoor (NWE) wearables: concepts

Region: Northern and Western Europe, stylised as **Mistmoor**: soft rain, mist on heather moors, peat bogs,
birch woods, rocky sea coasts. Climate trait: *soft rain, water lasts longer*. Craft sources: sheep's wool
(knitting, felting, weaving, tweed), waxed and oiled cloth, wood carving, wickerwork, birch bark, thatching,
plant dyes (woad blue, heather purple, tan-bark rust). Fauna: wren, robin, hare, puffin, eider duck, frog, snail, moth.
No flags, crests, clan patterns or costume pieces: every piece is built from materials, weather and plain craft.

Palettes use 6-tone ramps (dark to light), the `W.R` style of `gnome-wear/_gw.lua`. New ramps live in `common.lua`.
