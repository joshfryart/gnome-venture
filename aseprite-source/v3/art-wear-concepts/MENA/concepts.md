# MENA "The Dunewhisper" — wearable concepts (Hearth Pop v3)

Region: North Africa & the Middle East. Climate trait: **Dry heat: water lasts shorter, mulch matters more.**
Inspiration pool (materials and nature only, no symbols): sand dunes and stony hamada, oases, date palms,
Atlas cedar, damask roses, mint, pomegranates, gourds; camels, goats, fennec foxes, geckos, darkling
beetles, sandgrouse, hoopoes; crafts of soft-tanned leather, palm-fibre plaiting, flat-woven wool
(kilim-style stripes and diamonds), indigo and madder dyeing, cord braiding, hammered copper, quilting
against cold desert nights. Everything is a gentle gnome-sized take: plain shapes, honest materials.

Palettes are 6-tone ramps (dark -> light) in the `_gw.lua` style; new ramps live in `common.lua`
(`sand`, `ochre`, `indigo`, `madder`, `palm`, `cedar`, `copper`, `camel`, `goat`, `teal`, `gourd`,
`date`, `mint`, `turq`, `rose`, `linen`); the rest reuse `W.R` (`leather`, `strap`, `brass`, `wood`,
`water`, `canvas`, `lining`, `sole`).
