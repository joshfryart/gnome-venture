# Birchbramble Steppe (EE): wearable concepts

Region: wide grass steppe broken by birch groves and bramble hedges; short, hot summers (fast crops grow
faster) and long, snowy winters. Craft drawn on: birch-bark plaiting, bast and willow weaving, pressed felt,
sheepskin, hemp and linen with red thread stitching, flat-woven wool, beekeeping, sunflowers, poppies, wheat,
storks, hedgehogs, amber beads. Stylised for a travelling gnome: materials, plants, animals and plain craft
only (no national dress copied, no symbols, crests or flags).

Palettes are 6-tone dark -> light ramps (same style as `gnome-wear/_gw.lua` W.R); new ramps live in `common.lua`.
Outline ink `#2b1c26` on every icon.
