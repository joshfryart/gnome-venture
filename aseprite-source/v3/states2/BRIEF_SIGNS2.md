A first Opus pass made them good-looking but they are OVERPOWERING in the yard picture: the signs and plots are much bigger than the buildings they stand in for, and overlap the neighbours. The owner said: "Make the signs smaller. They are overpowering." Your job: shrink the empty state of each assigned building so it sits quietly in its spot, while keeping the charm and BUILD legibility.

SIZE BUDGET (hard limits, measure them from the alpha channel in your final PNG, frame 1 and all 4 frames)
- The WHOLE visible empty-state sprite (sign + mount + plot + stakes + life detail, excluding the soft cast shadow) must fit inside a box of 30 px wide x 26 px tall, and never be wider than the building's level-1 sprite footprint plus 4 px.
- The sign itself (plate plus its mount) at most 24 px wide x 20 px tall. The plate holds the word BUILD with a 2 px margin (about 23 x 9 px plate). The building icon becomes TINY (4x4 or 5x5 px, a clear recognisable glyph) sitting on the plate's end, on the mount top, or beside the word; if it does not fit legibly, put it as a little charm hanging or perched on the sign. BUILD must stay legible at 1x and 2x.
- The plot: a small cleared earth patch about the size of the level-1 building's footprint or smaller (flat, not raised), with four small corner stakes and a thin cream string line (1 px), 2 or 3 pebbles/tufts and at most one flower. No big boards, easels, chains, arches or large mounts any more: make the mount minimal (a short post, a peg, a single stake, a twig fork, a hook, a rope loop) but keep each building's sign DIFFERENT in silhouette and material from the others (see the list in BRIEF_SIGNS.md; keep your concept, scaled down and simplified: e.g. the kitchen chalkboard sits on a short two-leg stand; the beehive plaque hangs from a short bent hook; the arrow for the cellar stays a small arrow).
- The calm look matters more than detail: fewer colours (4 or 5 per sign), larger shapes, one tiny animated detail.

ANIMATION: still 4 frames looping; the sign sways or the hanging part swings by 1 px and ONE tiny life detail (a 2 to 4 px leaf, bird, bug or dust puff). The lettering must not jitter by more than 1 px.
