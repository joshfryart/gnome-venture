# Garden building state art: common brief (read fully)

You draw pixel-art STATE SPRITES for buildings of a cosy gnome garden game, with Aseprite Lua. Each building needs:

1. EMPTY (not yet bought), ANIMATED, 4 frames: a cleared plot of earth (an outlined footprint: corner stakes joined by a string line, a few stones/flowers fitting the building's world) with ONE SMALL SIGN that says BUILD. The word BUILD is drawn in a 3x5 pixel font (letters B U I L D, 1 px gap, so 19 px wide; draw your own glyphs; they must be legible at 4x zoom) on the sign, together with a tiny icon of that building. Every building has a DIFFERENT sign design (its own shape, mount and material, given in the table below), so the signs are never the same. Animation: the sign sways or flutters by 1 px, plus one small life detail (a leaf blowing past, a bird hopping, dust puff) on a 4-frame loop. No "!" badge on the empty state.
2. LEVEL 1, LEVEL 2, LEVEL 3: still frames showing the built building at each upgrade level (level info in the table). Level 1 is the current look of the building in the finished scene. Levels must be CLEARLY different at a glance (not just a recolour): add visible, logical parts per level. Each level may grow wider by up to 6 px (L2) and 12 px (L3), and taller by up to 8 px (L2) and 12 px (L3), centered on the anchor; never beyond the canvas. Do not let the sprite get busy: one clear silhouette, 3 to 5 added features per level at most.
3. READY animation, 4 frames, looping: the building has something for the player to collect/do (given in the table). Base art is the LEVEL 1 sprite; a gold "!" badge (round gold circle with an ink outline, about 9 px across, white-cream "!" inside) sits in the TOP-RIGHT corner of the canvas and bobs 0,-1,0,+1 px across the 4 frames; the building shows 2 or 3 visible life motions per the table (steam, sparkles, wobble, critters). The motion must be visible at 100% zoom.

## Canvas and anchor
Per building the canvas size (W x H native px) is in the table. Transparent background. The building's ground-contact centre (bottom centre of the footprint) sits at pixel (W/2, H-10) in EVERY piece so the pieces line up when swapped. Include the cool hard-edged cast shadow (lower-right) as part of every sprite.

## Style (Hearth Pop v3, must follow)
Rules file: /home/user/gnome-venture/aseprite-source/v3/art-style-guide-v3.md. Hard pixels only (no dither, gradients, semi-transparency); 1 px warm ink outline #2b1c26 round the whole object; light from the upper left, one shade side on the lower right; hue-shifted ramps; no pure black or white; 3/4 view with a lit front wall and a cooler side wall, roofs with two planes, eave shadow, recessed windows and doors, stone base courses, material texture (see the town-style "volume" recipe in the finished scene script). Colours must match the finished scene.
Library docs: /home/user/gnome-venture/aseprite-source/v3/lib/README.md (HP.canvas, c:part, c:rim, c:paint, c:save, HP.sheet; load with dofile("/home/user/gnome-venture/aseprite-source/v3/lib/hp.lua")).
FINISHED SCENE (the Level 1 reference): script /home/user/gnome-venture/aseprite-source/v3/scenes/garden-patch6.lua and its image /tmp/ase/out/garden-patch6.png (480x270; zoom with Python PIL, Image.NEAREST). Each building has its own drawing function in the script (named in the table) plus helper functions (volume kit: sideWall, hipRoof, paintRoof, window, door, plinth, cast, ball, cyl, ink, etc.). Copy and adapt what you need into your own script (they use a canvas variable c and global helpers R, E, px; define your own equivalents).
WORKED EXAMPLES of this exact job by an earlier pass (use their structure; fix their weaknesses: grey blob details, small differences between levels, faint effects): /home/user/gnome-venture/aseprite-source/v3/states/kitchen.lua and beehive.lua, and their outputs under /home/user/gnome-venture/aseprite-source/v3/states/kitchen-out and beehive-out (view the *-sheet.png with the Read tool). The earlier pass had no sign; yours must.

## Output
For each building <b> you are assigned: write the script to /tmp/ase/states2/<b>.lua and run
`/tmp/ase/squashfs-root/AppRun -b --script-param out=/tmp/ase/states2/<b>/ --script /tmp/ase/states2/<b>.lua`
(create the folder first). It must save into /tmp/ase/states2/<b>/ (scale 1, native pixels):
- <b>-empty.png : horizontal strip of the 4 frames (4W x H), frame 1 at x 0
- <b>-l1.png, <b>-l2.png, <b>-l3.png : single frames W x H
- <b>-ready.png : horizontal strip of the 4 frames (4W x H)
- matching .aseprite files (the strips with tags "empty" and "ready")
- <b>-sheet.png : a contact sheet at scale 3 on a plain light-green background showing empty frames 1-4, l1, l2, l3, ready frames 1-4 in one row (build it with Python PIL after the Aseprite run; python3 is available; use Image.NEAREST).
View the contact sheet with the Read tool, check: signs legible and different, levels clearly different, badge reads, nothing clipped, anchors aligned; fix and re-run, at most 4 rounds per building. Also verify no semi-transparent pixels (PIL: alpha only 0 or 255).
Do NOT touch any file under /home/user/gnome-venture. Do NOT touch another agent's building. Finish with ONE short line per building: done or what is weak.
