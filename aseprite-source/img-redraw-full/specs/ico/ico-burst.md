# ico/ico-burst
- canvas: 64x64, mode icon, 1x.
- subject: A comic-style impact burst: a spiky 10-point starburst in orange with a smaller cream star set inside it; loud, cheerful pop.
- must-read-at-16px: the spiky star outline with a pale cream core against the orange.
- distinct: two nested stars with uneven spike lengths (one long spike up-right, one long down-left), not a regular symmetric star or sun.
- parts:
  1. outer burst: 10-point star polygon, centre (32,32), tips out to about x 7..57, y 10..54, spike lengths uneven, base #e8892a (ember family #f08a24)
  2. inner star: same shape shrunk ~55%, centre (32,32), spans x 15..48, y 18..47, base #f8eed2 (pale, cream-safe)
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: spikes at least 3px wide at the base, tapering to a 1-2px point; cream inner star keeps an umber outline on every side (pale subject); gloss comma on the upper-left of the orange ring; crescent lower right.
