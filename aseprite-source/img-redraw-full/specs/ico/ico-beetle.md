# ico/ico-beetle
- canvas: 64x64, mode icon, 1x.
- subject: Top-down view of a glossy green beetle with a dark round head, six legs and two antennae; friendly toy.
- must-read-at-16px: an oval green shell with a dark head on top and legs splaying sideways.
- distinct: emerald-green shell with a dark centre seam, six splayed legs and a separate dark head circle.
- parts (back to front):
  1. six legs: 3px dark lines (19,30)->(11,25), (45,30)->(53,25), (18,39)->(10,41), (46,39)->(54,41), (20,48)->(13,55), (44,48)->(51,55), base #3a2f3a
  2. antennae: 3px lines (27,15)->(23,9), (37,15)->(41,9), 4x4 club tips, base #3a2f3a
  3. shell: ellipse (32,38) rx 15 ry 17, base #2f9a78 (emerald, run GV.ramp)
  4. wing seam: 3px vertical line x 32, y 27..54, #1d4f40
  5. head: circle (32,17) r7, base #3a2f3a (dusk dark #3e2f66 family)
- joins: the wing seam is the join; thread-gold dashes along it optional, skip over gloss.
- face: locked small eyes (3x3 disc, 2x2 pupil) on the head at (28,15) and (33,15).
- notes: shell is the hero; gloss on its upper-left. No spots.
