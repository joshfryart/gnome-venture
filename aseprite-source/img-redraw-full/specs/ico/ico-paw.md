# ico/ico-paw
- canvas: 64x64, mode icon, 1x.
- subject: An animal paw print, one big pad and four toe beans; friendly pet icon.
- must-read-at-16px: four toe blobs in an arch above one big round pad.
- distinct: classic paw with exactly four toes (two inner taller, two outer lower) and a wide main pad, in solid brown.
- parts:
  1. main pad: ellipse (32,42) rx14 ry12, #8c4a1a (wood shade #8a4f2a)
  2. outer left toe: ellipse (14,30) rx6 ry6, same brown
  3. inner left toe: ellipse (25,19) rx6.5 ry6.5
  4. inner right toe: ellipse (39,19) rx6.5 ry6.5
  5. outer right toe: ellipse (50,30) rx6 ry6
  6. gloss comma on each toe upper left, #d9a660
- joins: none. Toes sit at least 2 px clear of the pad (visible umber contour between).
- face: none.
- notes: toes must not merge into the pad at 16 px, keep 2-3 px gaps. Print is a lighter wood than the outline so it reads. No stitching.
