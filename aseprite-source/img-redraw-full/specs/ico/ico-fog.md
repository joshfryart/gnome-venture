# ico/ico-fog
- canvas: 64x64, mode icon, 1x.
- subject: Weather fog symbol: four staggered horizontal rounded bars of misty grey-blue, drifting left and right; calm and sleepy.
- must-read-at-16px: four stacked horizontal rounded bars with staggered left/right offsets.
- distinct: stacked pill bars of different lengths and offsets; no sun, cloud or droplets.
- parts:
  1. bar 1: pill x 14..50, y 11..17, base #9fb4bf (grey-blue, stone/water blend)
  2. bar 2: pill x 6..44, y 23..29 (shifted left)
  3. bar 3: pill x 20..58, y 35..41 (shifted right)
  4. bar 4: pill x 8..48, y 47..53
  5. bars are 7px tall, round ends (r 3), 5px gaps so halos merge cleanly
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: pale-ish blue-grey; gloss #fff3c8 but keep umber outline. Each bar is its own part with its own crescent. Offsets alternate to feel drifting.
