# ico/ico-link
- canvas: 64x64, mode icon, 1x.
- subject: Two interlocking chain links on a diagonal (upper right to lower left), one blue and one grey; the web-link / chain icon.
- must-read-at-16px: two linked pill rings crossing on a 45 degree diagonal, one blue, one grey.
- distinct: two stadium rings with open hollow centres overlapping in different colours; diagonal composition.
- parts (back to front):
  1. grey link: hollow stadium ring centre (40,24), long axis on the diagonal, about 30x18 px, ring thickness 5px, base #8b8f96 (stone)
  2. blue link: hollow stadium ring centre (24,40), same size and thickness, base #3f93b2 (water), overlapping the grey around (32,32)
  3. crossing: blue drawn over grey at one crossing, grey over blue at the other, 1px inner contours
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: hollow centres stay open (at least 4 px) so the halo shows through. Ring strands min 4 px. Over/under alternates for a true chain look.
