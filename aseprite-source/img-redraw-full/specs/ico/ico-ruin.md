# ico/ico-ruin
- canvas: 64x64, mode icon, 1x.
- subject: Ancient stone ruin: three broken columns/wall stubs of unequal height standing on a low slab with fallen blocks; crumbled and moody but toy-friendly.
- must-read-at-16px: a jagged tall central pillar with a broken stepped top flanked by two shorter broken stubs.
- distinct: crumbled broken-top skyline (peaks at y 9, 25, 31) with rubble blocks at the base; a pillar with a zigzag break is unique among building icons.
- parts:
  1. base slab: rrect x 8..56, y 50..57, #6a6070 (stone shade of #5b6470)
  2. left wall stub: polygon (10,50)(10,30)(14,26)(18,31)(22,25)(22,50), #968f96 (stone #8b8f96)
  3. centre pillar: polygon (25,50)(25,22)(29,13)(33,19)(37,9)(41,18)(41,50), #968f96, tallest, jagged top
  4. right wall stub: polygon (44,50)(44,36)(48,31)(52,38)(54,34)(54,50), #968f96
  5. dark inset blocks: rect (31,22,5x28) and (14,36,4x14), #6a6070 for crack/doorway look
  6. rubble: rects (8,46,8x6) #6a6070, (50,45,8x7) and (40,52,7x5) #968f96
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: columns at least 3px wide; 1px crack lines #2a1a10 down the pillar; light upper left, gloss on the pillar top. Fill the frame, outline all sides.
