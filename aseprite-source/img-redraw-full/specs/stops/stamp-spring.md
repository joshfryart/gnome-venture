# stops/stamp-spring
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker (round map-stop stamp), 1x. No text.
- subject: A circular passport-style ink stamp for a spring stop: a shallow bowl of water with three wavy steam wisps rising from it, inside a ring border; calm and fresh.
- must-read-at-16px: a round ring with a half-circle bowl and wavy wisps above.
- distinct: three tall S-curve wisps rising from a shallow bowl, plus a dotted outer ring; water-blue ring.
- parts:
  1. disc plate: circle (32,32) r29, base #e8d9b0 (pale, umber outline)
  2. dotted outer ring: 2px dots every 4px on r27, #3f8fb0 (water base)
  3. solid ring: r23 band 3px #3f8fb0; thin inner ring r20, 1px #8fcbd8
  4. bowl: half-ellipse x 20..44, y 36..46 (flat top at y36, round bottom), base #5a86b8; rim line 3px #2f6290 at y36
  5. three wisps: 3px S-curves at x 24, 32, 40, y 22..34, #8fcbd8 with #2f6290 shade side
  6. small drop: 3x3 dot at (36,40), #c8ecdc
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: plate stays pale, all ink in the water family; wisps min 2px with 3px gaps between them.
