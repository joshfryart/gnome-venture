# stops/stamp-mill
- canvas: 64x64, mode icon, 1x (map stop stamp, no text).
- subject: Round passport-style stamp of a windmill: a ring border around a small tapered tower mill with four crossed sails.
- must-read-at-16px: a round ring badge with a small X-shaped sail cross inside.
- distinct: rubber-stamp ring (thick ring plus dotted outer ring) around a tapered tower with four diagonal sails meeting at a hub.
- parts (back to front):
  1. disc: circle (32,32) r29, #c9a96e (soil pale)
  2. outer ring: 3px ring r26 and dotted ring r29, #5b6470 (stone)
  3. tower: trapezoid, base x 24..40 at y 46, top x 28..36 at y 28, #8b8f96 (stone)
  4. roof cap: triangle (28,28)(32,22)(36,28), #a8582f (wood)
  5. hub: 3px dot at (32,28), #2a1a10
  6. sails: four 2px diagonal bars from hub to (21,17),(43,17),(21,39),(43,39), #f1ece0 with umber outline
  7. door: 3x5 rect at (30,41), #5e2f1e
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: the X sails are the signature; keep the ring 3px thick so it holds at 16px. Original is a monochrome line stamp, so recolour with stone and wood families.
