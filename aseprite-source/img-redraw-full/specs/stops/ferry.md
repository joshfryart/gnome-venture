# stops/ferry
- canvas: 48x48, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: Map stop marker for a ferry: a small boat with a tall mast and two triangular sails floating on a wavy line of water; friendly travel toy.
- must-read-at-16px: a tall mast with a big triangular sail on its right, sitting on a flat hull.
- distinct: twin sails (large pale sail right of the mast, smaller warm sail left) over a trapezoid hull and a scalloped wave strip.
- parts:
  1. wave strip: scalloped band x 3..45, y 40..46, base #3f8fb0 (water)
  2. hull: trapezoid, top x 7..41 at y 31, bottom x 12..36 at y 39, base #b07a45 (wood)
  3. mast: 2 px pole x 23..24, y 6..32, #8a4f2a
  4. big sail: triangle (24,9),(37,27),(24,27), base #f4f0e0 (pale, cream-safe)
  5. small sail: triangle (24,12),(14,27),(24,27), base #e9b98a (pale warm)
  6. pennant: 3x3 #dd3a2a at the mast top
- joins: thread-gold seam along the hull top rim (x 9..39, y 32).
- face: none.
- notes: sails are pale so use cream-safe gloss and umber outline. Hull wider than the sails so it reads as a boat. Water family for waves, wood for hull.
