# stops/stamp-jetty
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x. Map-stop stamp; keep rings inside 3 px margin.
- subject: A round passport-style stamp of a little wooden jetty over choppy water with a hooked crane post; calm harbour mood. No text.
- must-read-at-16px: the flat deck on short legs over a wavy water line inside a round ring.
- distinct: a dashed outer ring plus a solid inner ring (stamp medallion) with a long deck on three posts and a leaning crane arm; no other stop uses a pier.
- parts:
  1. medallion disc: circle c(32,32) r28, base #f1ece0 (soil cream), ring band r25..28 in #b07a45 wood
  2. dashed outer ring: r29 dots 2 on / 1 off around the rim, #a8582f
  3. water: wavy band x 14..50, y 38..46, three scallop crests, #3f8fb0 with gloss #8fcbd8
  4. three posts: 3 px wide at x 20,27,34, y 33..44, #8a4f2a
  5. deck: rrect x 15..40, y 30..35, #b07a45
  6. crane arm: 3 px line (38,32) to (43,19), hook curve down to (40,29), #5e2f1e
  7. rope dot at (39,32), 2x2 #f5cf6a
- joins: seam along deck top edge x 17..38, y 31 (thread gold); no button.
- face: none.
- notes: ring is the stamp rim, may take edge stitch; posts must read as separate legs with water gaps. Wood ramp for deck, water ramp for waves.
