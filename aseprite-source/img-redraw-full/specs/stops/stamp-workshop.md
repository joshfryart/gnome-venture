# stops/stamp-workshop
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker (map stop stamp), 1x. No text.
- subject: A round passport stamp for the Workshop stop: a ring border with a dotted outer ring, and a gear in the middle over a flat bench line.
- must-read-at-16px: a round ring with a gear in the middle.
- distinct: 8-tooth gear over a bench baseline inside a double ring with a dotted outer ring; no figure or tool handle.
- parts (back to front):
  1. disc: circle (32,32) r26, pale cream #f1ece0 (pale rules)
  2. outer dotted ring: 2px dots every 4px on r29, #b07a45
  3. main ring: 2px ring r26, #8a4f2a (wood shade)
  4. thin inner ring: 1px r22, #b07a45
  5. gear teeth: 8 blocks 3x4 around (32,32) at r13 (diagonals 3x3), #b07a45
  6. gear body: circle (32,32) r8, #d9a660, hub hole circle r3 #5e2f1e
  7. bench line: 2px bar x 17..47, y 49, #8a4f2a
- joins: the dotted outer ring acts as the stitch.
- face: none.
- notes: ink-stamp feel in the wood/brown ramp; parts min 2px; gear is centred and big.
