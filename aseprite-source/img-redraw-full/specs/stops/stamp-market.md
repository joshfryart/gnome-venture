# stops/stamp-market
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x. Map-stop stamp.
- subject: A round passport-style stamp of a market stall with a scalloped awning, two posts and a counter holding fruit and a crate; busy and welcoming. No text.
- must-read-at-16px: a red scalloped awning over a counter inside a round ring.
- distinct: scalloped red awning (trapezoid roof), two upright posts and a goods counter with round fruit; the only stamp with an awning.
- parts:
  1. medallion disc: circle c(32,32) r28, #f1ece0, ring band r25..28 #b07a45
  2. dashed outer ring r29, 2 on / 1 off, #a8582f
  3. posts: two 3 px lines at x 21 and x 42, y 32..44, #8a4f2a
  4. counter board: rect x 19..45, y 36..40, #b07a45
  5. awning: trapezoid (19,28),(23,20),(41,20),(45,28), base #d6322b, scalloped lower edge with four bumps
  6. fruit: circles (27,34) r3 #e0501f and (33,34) r2.5 #f08a24
  7. crate: block 8x3 at (38,32), #c9a96e
- joins: thread-gold hem stitch along the awning scallop edge.
- face: none.
- notes: ring may take edge stitch; awning wider than the posts by 2 px each side.
