# gather/axe-bg
- canvas: 200x200, mode scene, draw at half-size (100x100), write 2x. Full-bleed, no halo.
- subject: Backdrop for a wood-chopping minigame: a soft green forest clearing with a tall trunk at each side, a round chopping stump at the bottom centre, warm light from the upper left; calm and sunny.
- must-read-at-16px: a round tan stump at the bottom centre on a green field framed by two dark trunks.
- distinct: a round cut-log stump with ring lines as the single focal prop, framed by two tall trunks with leafy canopies.
- parts (final px, 2x of the 100 grid):

  7. ground: wavy layer from y 150, #7fa85a, second layer from y 168, #4f7338; grass tufts #a8cf72 at x 20, 96, 170
  8. stump: side #8c6238, x 62..138, y 178..194 curved bottom; top ellipse (100,178) rx38 ry8.5 #d6aa6a; ring ellipses #b58348 rx30 and rx19; shadow ellipse (100,184) rx44
- joins: optional thread-gold hem stitch along the bottom edge.
- face: none.
- notes: bleeds all edges. Stump top is the brightest tan so the eye lands there. Three tiny #e4c274 pollen dots at (40,100),(150,86),(110,120). One light from the upper left.
