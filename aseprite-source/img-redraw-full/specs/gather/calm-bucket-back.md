# gather/calm-bucket-back
- canvas: 100x90, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: The back half of a wooden bucket seen slightly from above: tapered wooden tub with a dark rim and still blue-teal water at the top; the front half is drawn separately so the water stays open.
- must-read-at-16px: a wide tapered bucket with a dark oval opening holding blue water.
- distinct: back-wall view only: tall trapezoid body wider at the top, oval mouth with a dark wood rim ring and water ellipse inside; plank seams and two hoops.
- parts:
  1. ground shadow: ellipse (50,86) rx38 ry3.4, #5e2f1e (solid, no transparency)
  2. bucket body: trapezoid (10,18),(90,18),(82,82),(18,82), base #4a3320 (wood deep family)
  3. plank seams: 1px #2a1a10 lines at x~30, 50, 70 along the taper, y 24..80
  4. rim: ellipse (50,18) rx40 ry10, #3a2716
  5. water: ellipse (50,19) rx35 ry7.6, #1f3a48 (water deep family); 1px lighter ring #3f8fb0 at upper-left
  6. hoops: two 3px bands across the body at y 40 and y 66, following the curve, #7a3a14
- joins: optional thread-gold stitch dashes along the hoops.
- face: none.
- notes: this is the BACK layer, keep the mouth dark and calm; wood warm brown, water is the only cool colour.
