- subject: A butterfly-style hand net: round mesh hoop on a short diagonal wooden handle; light and breezy.

- joins: seam on the handle at the ferrule, 2 thread stitches; edge stitch on hoop lit half.
- face: none.
- notes: keep whole object 3 px inside frame (original handle ran to the corner, shorten it). Mesh stays lighter than hoop.
