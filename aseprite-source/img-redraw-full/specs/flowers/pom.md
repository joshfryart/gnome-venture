# flowers/pom
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A round pompom dahlia seen from above: dense ball of many small petal-scoops spiralling into a tight centre; cheerful, plump felt flower.
- must-read-at-16px: a big round scalloped flower disc with a darker spiral core.
- distinct: three rings of rounded scoop petals (12 outer, 8 middle, 5 inner) stepping smaller and darker toward the centre, giving a scalloped round silhouette; no stem, no leaves, no centre disc.
- parts (back to front):

  2. middle ring: 8 circles r6.5 on a radius-12 ring, rotated 15 deg off the outer ring, base #e46a9b, 1px inner contours
  3. inner ring: 5 circles r5 on a radius-5.5 ring, deeper petal #c9507f (GV.ramp of #e46a9b, shade)
  4. core: circle (32,32) r3, #b8386b
  5. glints: 1px cream dots on lit outer petals near (24,22),(38,14),(17,30),(40,26),(30,38)
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: one petal family (#e46a9b, light #f6b8cf); upper-left petals take gloss, lower-right petals take crescents. Edge stitch optional on the outer ring only. Scalloped round outline is the identity.
