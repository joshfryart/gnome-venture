# flowers/star
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x. Flower head only, no stem; tintable petal colour, default petal #e46a9b.
- subject: A five-point star-shaped blossom, long narrow pointed petals radiating from a golden centre; bright and cheerful.
- must-read-at-16px: five separate spiky petals radiating like a star around a gold dot.
- distinct: petals are long thin ellipses 72 degrees apart with gaps between tips; NOT round overlapping petals.
- parts:
  1. five petals: ellipses 16 wide x 31 long, centred 15 px from the middle, rotated 0,72,144,216,288 about (32,32), base #e46a9b
  2. petal crease: 2 px darker line down each petal inner half, petal shade
  3. petal highlight: 1x3 light dash near each tip, #f6b8cf
  4. centre disc: circle c(32,32) r6, base #f5cf6a, rim #e8a317
  5. centre glint: 2x2 at (30,30) #fff1c8
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: one petal points up; keep tips 4 px inside the frame. Gaps between petals stay visible (notches at 2 px).
