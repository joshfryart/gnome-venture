# flowers/orchid
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x. Draw petals in mid magenta-pink #e46a9b (petal family).
- subject: A moth orchid bloom seen from the front: five broad petals fanned around a frilly spotted lip with a tiny pale column; elegant.
- must-read-at-16px: wide spread of petals with a pale lip below a bright dot centre.
- distinct: two very wide horizontal side petals, one tall top petal and two drooping lower petals plus a spotted lip; broad asymmetric silhouette unlike round daisies.
- parts (back to front):
  1. lower petals: ellipses (22,46) and (42,46) rx6 ry12, tilted 32 deg outward, base #e46a9b
  2. side petals: wide ellipses (15,28) and (49,28) rx15 ry9.5, tilted -18/+18 deg, base #e46a9b; pale streak #f6b8cf on each
  3. top petal: ellipse (32,15) rx8.5 ry12.5, base #e46a9b; darker centre streak
  4. lip: ellipse (32,42) rx10 ry9, base #e46a9b; pale inner #f6b8cf rx6.5 ry6; darker lower #b8456f
  5. lip spots: five 2px dots #8a2f62 on the lip
  6. column: circle (32,31) r4.5, #f1ece0; yellow dot (32,31) r2 #f1b92d (gold)
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: pale highlights cream-safe. Petals overlap with inner contours. Min 2px parts.
