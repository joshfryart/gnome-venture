# flowers/sprig-fern
- canvas: 64x96, mode sticker, 1x (tall).
- subject: A single fern frond: a thin central stem with pairs of narrow pointed leaflets angling up and out; fresh and light.
- must-read-at-16px: a vertical stem with a feathery zigzag of alternating leaflets.
- distinct: tall one-stem frond, leaflets alternating left/right at about 62 degrees and shrinking toward the tip; no pot or ground.
- parts:
  1. stem: 3px line from (32,90) curving to (32,10), base #3b8250
  2. leaflets right: 6 pointed ovals 3px wide, 12-18px long, rooted every ~9px from y 84 to y 26, angled up-right, base #79b86a
  3. leaflets left: 6 matching leaflets offset half a step, angled up-left, base #4f9d5d
  4. tip leaflet: 3px wide, 8px long at (32,10), #79b86a
  5. midrib: 1px #3b8250 down each leaflet (skip if under 14px)
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: leaflets shrink from 18px to 8px going up; stay 4px inside frame top and bottom; two greens alternate sides so the zigzag reads.
