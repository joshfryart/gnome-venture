# flowers/lily
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x. Petal colour is a tint slot (original used currentColor): use petal pink family #e46a9b / #f6b8cf.
- subject: Top-down lily bloom with six pointed petals, three orange stamens and a green centre.
- must-read-at-16px: six-pointed star silhouette with a small orange-dotted centre.
- distinct: two interleaved rings of 3 petals (outer 3 at 90/210/330 degrees shorter and darker, inner 3 at 30/150/270 longer, lighter), pointed lily tips, NOT round daisy petals.
- parts:
  1. outer petals x3 pointing up, lower-left, lower-right (reach r~28 from centre (32,32)), base #e46a9b with deeper #b8452f-ish shade, shade 12%
  2. inner petals x3 pointing upper-right, lower-right... at 30/150/270 deg, reach r~31, base #f6b8cf rim, 1px central vein line #e46a9b
  3. stamens x3: 1.5px filaments from centre 10-12px out at 30/150/270, #b7791f; anthers 5x3 ellipses #e8891a at tips
  4. centre: circle r3 at (32,32) #8bbf5a (moss); six 1px maroon freckle dots #7a2e4a around radius 6
  5. gloss: 1px x 8px light streak on each inner petal
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: petal gaps must show between points; centre cluster at least 6px wide.
