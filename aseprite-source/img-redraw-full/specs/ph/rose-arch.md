# ph/rose-arch
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A flat wooden trellis frame: three upright posts crossed by two horizontal rails, a garden support for climbing roses.
- must-read-at-16px: a grid of three vertical posts and two horizontal bars with open gaps.
- distinct: ladder-like lattice with open windows between posts rather than a curved arch; rails lighter than posts.
- parts (back to front):
  1. posts: three 5px bars at x 12..16, 30..34, 48..52, y 14..56, #8c4a1a (wood dark)
  2. upper rail: rounded bar x 8..56, y 22..28, #c4925f (wood light)
  3. lower rail: rounded bar x 8..56, y 38..44, #c4925f
  4. 1px cream edge on the rails, #f0d49a
- joins: optional 4x4 gold button at the post/rail crossing (30,23); no seams.
- face: none.
- notes: gaps between posts 5+ px so the halo does not fill them solid; posts pass behind rails; gloss on the upper rail (22,23).
