# ph/pear
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A classic green pear with a narrow neck, a round bulbous bottom, a short brown stem and one leaf.
- must-read-at-16px: pear silhouette, narrow top and wide round bottom.
- distinct: tall pear silhouette (neck width ~14, base width ~34) with a curved brown stem tilting right; unlike every round fruit.
- parts (back to front):
  1. lower bulb: circle (32,40) r16, base #7ab55c (moss base)
  2. upper neck: rounded trapezoid x 24..40, y 12..32 joining the bulb, same #7ab55c
  3. stem: 3px, from (32,13) curving to (36,7), #8a4f2a (wood shade)
  4. leaf: small teardrop at (37,10) pointing right to (46,8), #58a03e
  5. blossom end: 3px dark dot (32,55), #4a8a3e
  6. gloss comma on the bulb (25,35) and dot; lower-right shade crescent
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: moss ramp gloss #c8d77a, shade #4a8a3e. No inner seam; neck/bulb join is a smooth S curve.
