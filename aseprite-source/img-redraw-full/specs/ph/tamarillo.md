# ph/tamarillo
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A glossy pink-crimson tamarillo (tree tomato) with a green stalk and one leaf; plump and juicy.
- must-read-at-16px: magenta-pink egg-shaped fruit with a green stalk and leaf above.
- distinct: egg-shaped (taller than wide) pink body with a wine-dark lower half, a longer stalk and a pointed bottom nub; unlike the round lime and tangelo.

  4. stalk: (32,21)-(32,14), 3px wide #2f5a2a
  5. leaf: pointed blade (33,18) curving to (38,6), #58a03e, midrib #4a8a3e
  6. tip nub: 2x2 dot #6b1b2c at (32,52)
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: gloss comma at (25,28); the pale streak is a 1-2px accent only; shade crescent lower right deep wine.
