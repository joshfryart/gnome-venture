# ph/gathering-pouch
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A soft cloth foraging pouch, round-bellied and cinched at the neck with a drawstring, ready to be filled with wild finds. Cosy, humble.
- must-read-at-16px: squat round-bottomed sack with a puckered neck and a looped cord on top.
- distinct: gathered ruffle neck with a thread-gold cord tied in a bow, belly bulging wider than the neck (no rigid rim, unlike the trug).
- parts (back to front):
  1. belly sack: rounded teardrop x 12..52, y 26..56, wide at y~44, base #b07a10 (gold shade family #b8661a / wood #b07a45)
  2. neck ruffle: 3 puckered lobes x 20..44, y 20..30, base #c4925f (wood rim #d9a660)
  3. cord band: 2px band across the neck y 28..30, x 18..46, #f5cf6a (thread gold)
  4. bow: two 4x4 loops at (26..30, 24..28) and (34..38, 24..28) plus 2px tails down to y 36, #8c4a1a (wood family #8a4f2a)
  5. fold lines: three 1px short curves on the belly (22,40) (32,46) (26,52), #5e2f1e
- joins: gold drawstring band is the seam (2 on / 1 off); 4x4 button optional at the bow knot (30,28).
- face: none.
- notes: edge stitch on the belly lit half. Gloss on upper-left of belly (23,40). Warm tan fabric, no logo.
