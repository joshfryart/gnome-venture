# ph/npc-holly-barkley

- subject: Portrait of Holly Barkley the field mouse: sweet brown mouse with big round ears and a cream muzzle, wearing a gold jacket with a purple neckerchief.

- distinct: purple neckerchief knot with a dangling tail triangle at the right of the chest, and whiskers drawn out past the cheeks.

- face: locked eyes (5x5 cream, 3x3 pupil) at (31,49) and (49,49).
