# ph/cherimoya
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A cherimoya (custard apple), a heart-shaped green-orange fruit covered in soft scale bumps, with a short stem and one leaf; friendly toy fruit.
- must-read-at-16px: a wide heart/cone body, fat at the top and tapering to a rounded point at the bottom, with a scaled bump texture.
- distinct: heart-shaped taper with rows of small U-scale bumps; not a round ball like cherry/coffee.
- parts:
  1. body: heart/cone x 16..48, y 20..54, wide top (two shoulder lobes at y 22), tip at (32,54), base #f08a24 (ember family; upper half shifted toward moss #a4b86a)
  2. scale bumps: 3 rows of 3-4 small 3px U-scallops (y 28, 36, 44), 1px #e0501f lines, each row offset
  3. stem: 3x8 bar x 30..33, y 12..20, #2f5a2a
  4. leaf: pointed oval x 34..46, y 8..18, tilted up-right, #7ab55c, 1px vein #2f5a2a
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: gloss on the upper-left shoulder; moss-to-orange change is a hard 2-tone split, no dither.
