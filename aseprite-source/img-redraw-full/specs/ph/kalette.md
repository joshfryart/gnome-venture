# ph/kalette

  3. mid leaves: ring circle (32,31) r13 offset lobes, #6a9a3a, 2px dark gaps #2f5a2a between lobes
  4. core: small circle (32,30) r5, #3e2f66 (dusk) with rim #7b66b4
  5. 2 purple edge frill tips at (14,28) and (50,36), #7b66b4
- joins: none.
- face: none.
