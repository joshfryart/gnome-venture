# ph/leech-slime
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A glistening teardrop blob of green slime shed by a leech, pooling at the base; slightly gross but cute.
- must-read-at-16px: green teardrop with a pointed tip at top and a round heavy bottom.
- distinct: tall pointed drop with a small bubble beside it and a flat darker puddle disc underneath, unlike round fruit.
- parts (back to front):
  1. puddle disc: ellipse (32,46) rx 12 ry 4, x 20..44, y 42..50, #4a8a3e (moss shade)
  2. drop body: teardrop, tip (32,8), widest x 14..50 at y 42, rounded bottom to y 56; base #7ab55c (moss)
  3. ripple line: 1px curve from (18,27) to (46,25), #2f5a2a
  4. bubble: 4x4 disc at (40,24), #c8d77a (moss gloss), 1px #2f5a2a edge
  5. big gloss comma upper-left (24,17)
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: wet, so gloss must be the brightest thing; no eyes. Not a water drop: green and heavier at the bottom.
