# ph/walnut
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker (halo 5), 1x.
- subject: A whole walnut in its shell with a tiny green stem nub, plump and wrinkly.
- must-read-at-16px: round-ish brown oval with a dark top cap and a green nub on top.
- distinct: walnut shell with vertical ridge grooves and a curved smile-like seam on the lower front; green stem nub rising above a darker cap lid.
- parts (back to front):
  1. shadow: ellipse (32,57) rx17 ry3.5, #6a4528
  2. shell: oval (32,36) rx15 ry17 (x17..47, y19..53), base #a5663a (wood #a8582f/#b07a45)
  3. top cap: lens x17..47, y22..30 arching up to y~24, base #8c4a1a (wood #8a4f2a)
  4. stem nub: 3px wide rect (31,14)->(33,20), base #2e5a2c (moss #2f5a2a)
  5. front seam: curved 2px line (26,42) dipping to (32,45) up to (38,42), #2a1a10
  6. ridges: 1px #5e2f1e short vertical dashes at (26,26..32), (34,37..43), (41,29..35)
- joins: front seam is the shell join (thread-gold stitch 2 on / 1 off along it, optional).
- face: none.
- notes: wood ramp; gloss comma on upper-left (26,28); lower-right shade crescent. Cap must be visibly darker than shell.
