# ph/pigeon-pea
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A curved, plump green pea pod lying on a diagonal, three round peas bulging out of it, small stem tail at lower left; fresh and cheerful.
- must-read-at-16px: three round bumps in a row along a curved green pod rising to the upper right.
- distinct: crescent-moon pod arching upward, with three separate round pea bumps standing proud of the pod edge (not a flat bean).
- parts:

  2. pod ridge: 2px curved line along the upper inside, (14,38)->(50,22), #4a8a3e (moss shade)
  3. three peas: discs r6 at (24,32),(33,28),(42,25), overlapping pod top edge, base #a4b86a (moss rim) lighter than pod, 1px inner contour each
  4. stem tail: 3px stub (10,40)->(6,45), #2f5a2a (moss deep)
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: peas must visibly bulge above the pod outline; gloss on the first pea at upper left; green only, no cream except halo.
