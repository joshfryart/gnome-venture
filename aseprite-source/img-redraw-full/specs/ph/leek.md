# ph/leek
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A single leek lying diagonally, pale green-white stalk with a dark leafy tip and white roots; a vegetable ingredient.
- must-read-at-16px: long diagonal stalk from lower-left to upper-right with a dark green blade end.
- distinct: one long thin diagonal stalk (about 12 px thick), pale lower half and dark blade tip, plus a tuft of white roots at the lower-left; no round shapes.
- parts (back to front):
  1. roots: fan of 2px cream lines at the lower-left end around (10,50), #f1ece0 (soil pale)
  2. stalk: rounded bar from (10,46) to (50,18), width 12; base #a4b86a (moss rim)
  3. lower shade half: lower-right half of the stalk #7ab55c
  4. blade end: darker rounded bar at upper-right (46..58, 12..24), #2f5a2a (moss deep)
  5. gloss comma on the stalk near (24,34)
- joins: optional single thread-gold band across the stalk at (36,30).
- face: none.
- notes: lemongrass is a similar bar, so keep the leek pale-green with dark tip and roots; no gold.
