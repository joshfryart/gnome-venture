# ph/wattle-fence

  3. lower weave rod: band y 38..45, behind stake 1, in front of 2, behind 3, base #c4925f
  4. rod ends: 3px stubs poking out at x 7 and x 57
  5. ground tufts: 4px #7ab55c blades at the feet (12,56),(48,56)
- joins: none (weave crossings act as joins).
- face: none.
- notes: wood family plus tiny moss feet. Rods min 4px thick. Rod colour alternates light/dark at each crossing.
