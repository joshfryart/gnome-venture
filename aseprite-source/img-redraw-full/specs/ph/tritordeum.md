# ph/tritordeum
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A golden hybrid cereal ear on a stalk, grains in gold and amber, with a green leaf at the base.
- must-read-at-16px: tall stalk with a paired gold / amber grain ear.
- distinct: GOLD (#e8a317) left grains and BROWN-AMBER (#b07a10) right grains, so the ear reads warm and yellow, unlike pink-red triticale.
- parts (back to front):
  1. stalk: 3px line x32 y24..58, #2e5a2c (moss deep #2f5a2a)
  2. left grains: tilted ovals (-25deg) at (25,22), (25,29), (25,36), ~9x14, #e8a317 (gold base)
  3. right grains: ovals (+25deg) at (39,22), (39,29), (39,36), #b07a10 (gold bounce #d0862a to shade #b8661a)
  4. tip grain: vertical oval (32,15) 9x16, #e8a317; make it slightly taller than on triticale
  5. leaf: slim leaf (32,52) swinging up-left to (24,41), #58a03e (moss #6a9a3a)
  6. crescent shades lower right; 1px umber contour where grains overlap
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: gloss comma on upper-left gold grain (24,19) in #fff1c8.
