# ph/creature-dung-beetle
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker (halo 5, outline 2px+1).
- subject: A brown dung beetle seen from above pushing a big round ball at its right; round shiny shell, small dark head and six small legs.
- must-read-at-16px: a brown beetle body at lower left with a big round brown ball beside it at upper right.
- distinct: the large pushed ball (13 px across) beside the beetle gives a diagonal beetle-and-ball pair, unlike the plain bug siblings.
- parts (back to front):
  1. legs: three 2 px strokes per side #2a1a10: left (25,41)->(15,37), (25,45)->(15,47), (25,49)->(15,56); mirrored right from x39 to x49
  2. shell: ellipse (32,46) rx10.5 ry12, #8c4a1a (wood #8a4f2a)
  3. wing split: 1 px line x32 y35..57 #44281c
  4. head: circle (32,32) r5, #44281c
  5. antennae: 2 px strokes (30,28)->(24,23) and (34,28)->(40,23), 4x4 clubs
  6. ball: circle (45,32) r6.5, #8c4a1a with a lighter arc #a5663a on its upper-left
- joins: the wing split is a thread-gold seam, 2 on 1 off.
- face: head about 10 px wide: 3x3 eye discs with 2x2 pupils at (29,31) and (35,31).
- notes: make the ball one step lighter than the shell so they separate; contact shadow under the ball where it touches the beetle. Dark head needs a lit rim.
