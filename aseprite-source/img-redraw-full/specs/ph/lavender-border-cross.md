# ph/lavender-border-cross
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x. Arms stop about 4 px from the frame.
- subject: A plus-shaped crossing of two garden borders of green foliage topped with purple lavender spikes.
- must-read-at-16px: a green plus with a purple speckled top.
- distinct: only this cross has two-tone purple flower tufts (dark #7a5cc0 and light #c3b2ea) in staggered rows along both arms.
- parts:
  1. shadow strip #6a4528
  2. horizontal border: x 4..60, y 24..40, base #58a03e (moss #6a9a3a)
  3. vertical border: x 24..40, y 4..60, same green
  4. dark lavender tufts: 3x5 vertical clumps #7a5cc0 in a row along y 27..31 (x 6,12,18,24,36,42,48,54) and along x 27..31 (y 8,15,22,43,50,56)
  5. light lavender tufts: 3x4 clumps #c3b2ea offset 2 px right/down of the dark row, same spacing
  6. green ridge: 1 px #b8d47c under the tufts
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: tufts are small vertical spikes 3 px wide; alternate dark and light so the bar looks speckled, not striped. Pale lilac gets umber outline.
