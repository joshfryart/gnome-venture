# ph/wind-chime

- subject: A wind chime: a sagging string with three pointed orange tubes dangling; breezy and musical.
- must-read-at-16px: three downward-pointing triangles hanging from a curved line.
- distinct: curved hanging string with three staggered pennants of different lengths, no body or container.
- parts (back to front):

  2. string: arc from (6,14) dipping to (32,22) up to (58,14), 2-3 px thick #4a2c1a
  3. left chime: triangle (14,20),(19,32),(24,22) widened to 8 px wide, #f08a24 (ember)
  4. centre chime: triangle (27,25),(32,37),(37,25), #e8a317 (gold), longest
  5. right chime: triangle (42,22),(47,34),(52,20), #f08a24
- joins: none; 1 px thread-gold tie at each chime top.
- face: none.
- notes: all pieces at least 3 px wide; staggered tips y32,37,34. Gloss on upper-left of each chime, shade lower right. Keep 3 px inside frame.
