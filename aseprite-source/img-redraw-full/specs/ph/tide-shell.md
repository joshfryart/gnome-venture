- subject: A pink tide shell, a faceted hexagonal shell with a groove down the middle and an inner ring of darker facets; beach treasure.

  4. pearl dots: three 1px accent dots (26,25),(35,32),(30,39) #f0d49a thread-cream
- joins: none.
- face: none.
