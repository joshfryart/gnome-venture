# ph/raspberry
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A plump raspberry, a rounded thimble of tiny bead-like drupelets with a flat green star calyx on top; ripe and cheerful. Wide dome with bead bumps, no pointed tip.
- must-read-at-16px: a rounded pink-red dome with a bumpy bead-cluster outline and a green star cap.
- distinct: a squat dome (wider than tall) whose edge is scalloped by individual beads, with a dark dimple at the base. NOT the strawberry's pointed heart.
- parts:
  1. berry body: ellipse centre (32,39) rx 19, ry 16, base #e46a9b (petal), lower-right half #a8241f
  2. drupelet beads: 12 circles r4 in 3 offset rows (y 28, 36, 45; x spaced 9), alternating #e46a9b / #a8241f, 1px umber contours between them
  3. base dimple: ellipse (32,53) rx 7, ry 2, #6b1b2c
  4. calyx: 5-point flat star centre (32,22) radius 10, pointed leaves, base #58a03e (moss, shade #2f5a2a)
  5. stem nub: rect x 30..34, y 12..19, #4a8a3e
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: gloss on upper-left bead. Beads at least 7px each. Colour families petal + red. Keep calyx star flat on top of the dome.
