# ph/creature-paper-wasp
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A chibi paper wasp: round black head with big eyes, a striped golden abdomen below, two large translucent pale wings spread behind, and curling antennae.
- must-read-at-16px: yellow-and-black striped abdomen under a dark head with two pale wings spread sideways.
- distinct: two wide pale-mint wings spread horizontally behind the head and three curved black stripes on the golden abdomen; the only banded insect.
- parts (back to front):
  1. ground shadow (32,57) rx18
  2. wings: two ellipses (21,32.8) rx10.8 ry6.3 and (43,32.8) rx10.8 ry6.3, #e6f1ec (pale, cream-safe)
  3. abdomen: ellipse (32,43.6) rx10.8 ry12.6 #e8a317 (gold)
  4. stripes: three 3 px curved bands #2a1a10 at y 40, 46, 52, x 24..40 (smile curves)
  5. stinger: 2 px line (32,55)->(32,60) #2a1a10
  6. head: circle (32,29) r7.2 #2a1a10; face patch ellipse (32,32) rx3.6 ry2.7 #e8a317
  7. antennae: 1.5 px curls (29,22)->(24,17) and (35,22)->(40,17) #2a1a10, club tips 4x4
- joins: none.
- face: locked eyes (white-glint style) at (24,25) and (35,25); head 14 px wide, so use small 3x3 disc with 2x2 pupil.
- notes: black head uses umber outline lighter variant so the outline reads; wings pale cream-safe.
