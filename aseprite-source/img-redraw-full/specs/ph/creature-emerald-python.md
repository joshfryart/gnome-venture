# ph/creature-emerald-python
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A chubby emerald-green python coiled in a fat C-shaped loop with its head raised on the left, tongue flicking; calm, a bit sly.
- must-read-at-16px: thick green coil ring with a round raised head on the left.
- distinct: Diamond-patterned back (dark green lozenges) plus a cream belly stripe along the coil; tight ring coil with a raised neck, unlike the grass snake's plain banded S-curve.
- parts (back to front):
  1. coil ring body: thick ring ~10 px wide, outer x 8..56, y 36..58, hole around (33,46); base #7ab55c (moss base)
  2. belly stripe: 3 px cream line following the ring centre, #f8eed2 (cream-safe)
  3. back diamonds: six 5x5 diamonds at (21,56) (31,57) (41,55) (49,49) (41,42) (29,42), #4a8a3e (moss shade)
  4. neck: curved 9 px tube from (13,49) up to (15,33), #7ab55c
  5. head: circle centre (16,29) r8.5 plus snout bulge to (32,33), #7ab55c
  6. tongue: 1 px forked #d6322b from (23,33) to (30,36), fork at the tip
  7. mouth: 1 px #2a1a10 curve (18,33)-(22,33)
- joins: none (optional thread-gold seam down the neck, 2 on 1 off).
- face: locked eyes at (10,25) and (17,25); head is ~17 px wide so use the small 3x3 disc with 2x2 pupil.
- notes: green stays emerald (moss family); tongue is the only red. Ground shadow ellipse (32,57) rx20 ry3.
