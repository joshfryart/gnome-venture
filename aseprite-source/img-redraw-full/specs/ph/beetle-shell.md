# ph/beetle-shell
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A discarded beetle wing-case, a pointed shield-like shell split down the middle with a ridged inner rim; glossy purple-blue.
- must-read-at-16px: faceted gem-like shield with a vertical centre split.
- distinct: angular hexagonal shield with a pointed top and bottom (not round), centre split line, and a nested inner facet outline.
- parts (back to front):
  1. outer shell: hexagon-ish poly (32,8),(54,20),(50,46),(32,56),(14,46),(10,20), base #7a5cc0 (dusk rim #7b66b4)
  2. inner facet: smaller poly (32,14),(46,22),(43,40),(32,47),(21,40),(18,22), #4e3a8a
  3. shade crescent: lower-right #3e2f66, deep #291d4a
  4. centre split: 2px line x 31..32, y 8..52, #291d4a
  5. ridge arcs: two 1px scalloped rows at y 30, x 18..36 and x 24..40, #a894dc
- joins: centre split doubles as the seam; optional thread-gold stitch on the split would be wrong (it is a shell), so none.
- face: none.
- notes: gloss comma upper-left on the left half; both halves slightly asymmetric.
