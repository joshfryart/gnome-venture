# ph/cork-bark
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A short curled log-section of cork oak bark lying on its side, rough brown outside with a pale cut end showing a ring; calm, humble craft material.
- must-read-at-16px: a horizontal brown tube with a pale oval end-cap on the right.
- distinct: a fat horizontal rolled-bark tube with a visible round cut face and a ring inside it (no leaves, no stem), unlike the upright plants.
- parts:
  1. bark tube: pill x 6..58, y 24..48 (r12), base #8c4a1a (wood #8a4f2a/#a8582f)
  2. cut end face: ellipse centre (50,36) rx9 ry12, base #dcb06a (wood rim #d9a660)
  3. growth ring: ellipse (50,36) rx5 ry7, 2px ring #a5663a
  4. furrow grooves: two 2px dashes on the tube, x14..34 y30 and x12..36 y40, #44281c (wood deep #5e2f1e)
  5. nubbly bark flecks: three 2px darker bumps along the lower edge, #5e2f1e
- joins: none (no stitch on wood).
- face: none.
- notes: wood ramp gloss #ecc58a on upper-left of tube; end face is the lightest area. Keep the two grooves separate and unequal in length.
