# ph/starlight-hammock
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker (halo 5), 1x.
- subject: A snug fabric hammock slung between two short posts with a couple of small gold stars stitched on it, restful bedtime item.
- must-read-at-16px: sagging cloth between two upright posts.
- distinct: drooping U-shaped cloth hung between two posts with a pair of gold stars, unlike rigid furniture.
- parts (back to front):
  1. posts: two 5x34 uprights at x8..12 and x52..56, y22..56, #8a4f2a
  2. hammock cloth: smile-curve x10..54, ends y28, sag to y42, base #5a86b8 (water bounce)
  3. cloth lower band: y36..42, #3f8fb0
  4. stars: two 4x4 plus-stars on cloth at (24,34), (40,36), #f5cf6a
  5. ropes: 1px lines from post tops to cloth ends, #d9a660
  6. lower-right crescent: #2f6290 then #2a3c6c
- joins: edge stitch along the cloth rim (1px thread gold 2 on / 1 off).
- face: none.
- notes: felt cloth, so edge stitch allowed; gloss on upper-left (20,32). Posts get wood shade crescents.
