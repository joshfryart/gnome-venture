# ph/paper-comb
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A flat paper hair-comb, a green card strip with a straight top edge and a row of comb teeth hanging down; humble craft item.
- must-read-at-16px: rectangle with a zigzag/toothed bottom edge.
- distinct: bottom edge is a row of 5 pointed teeth with notches between; the only flat card-strip-with-teeth silhouette among the ph items.
- parts (back to front):
  1. card body: rect x 10..54, y 16..42, base #7ab55c (moss base)
  2. teeth: 5 triangles hanging from y 42 down to y 50, each ~8 px wide, tips at x 14,23,32,41,50, notches at y 45, same #7ab55c, min 3 px wide at root
  3. paper lines: two 2px cream bars x 18..46 at y 24 and y 32, one x 18..38 at y 40, #d6c9b6 (pale, cream-safe)
  4. gloss comma upper-left (23,25); shade crescent along the lower edge of the card above the teeth
- joins: optional edge stitch 1px, 2px inside card edge, thread gold, skipped over gloss.
- face: none.
- notes: moss ramp gloss #c8d77a, shade #4a8a3e, deep #2f5a2a. Teeth must not look like a fence; notches at least 2px deep.
