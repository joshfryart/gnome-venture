# ph/land-mountain
- canvas: 64x64, mode scene (tile, full-bleed rounded square, no halo), 1x.
- subject: Map-tile landscape: sunny sky over a grey twin-peaked mountain with snow caps, a green meadow strip and two small pine trees; calm and cheerful.
- must-read-at-16px: tall grey peak with a white cap, green strip below, gold sun at upper left.
- distinct: snow-capped zigzag peak plus a gold sun disc in the sky corner; no building or path.
- parts (back to front):
  1. sky tile: rrect x 3..60, y 3..60 (r7), #a9d6ea (water family #c8ecdc/#8fcbd8), 3-4 hard sky bands
  2. sun: circle (15,15) r5, #f5cf6a, 2px gloss #fff1c8 at (13,13)
  3. mountain: peak apex (22,20), base y50 from x3 to x41; second lower peak apex (42,26) down to x61 at y50, notch at (34,36); #968f96 (stone #8b8f96), right-side shade #5b6470
  4. snow caps: zigzag cap under (22,20) down to y~30 and smaller one under (42,26) to y~34, #f4f0e0 (cream-safe)
  5. ridge line: 1px #6a6070 from (22,20) to (29,34) to (22,50)
  6. meadow: strip y 50..60, rounded bottom corners, #58a03e (moss #7ab55c)
  7. pine left: trunk x13..15 y50..55 #b07a45; stacked triangles apex (14,42) base y51 w9 #2f5a2a, and apex (14,47) w7 #4a8a3e
  8. pine right: same at x~50 (apex (50,45), base y52), slightly smaller
- joins: optional thread-gold hem stitch along the meadow bottom edge.
- face: none.
- notes: tile fills the frame; 2px umber edge #4a2c1a around the tile. Gloss only on sun and snow.
