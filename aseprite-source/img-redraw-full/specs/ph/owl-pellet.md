# ph/owl-pellet
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: An owl pellet, a lumpy grey-brown oval of fur and tiny bones coughed up by an owl, with a few small bone bits poking out; slightly gross but cute.
- must-read-at-16px: a fat grey-brown lump with a tiny pale bone sticking out.
- distinct: lumpy horizontal oval with fur tufts and two cream bone fragments poking out (a rib curve and a tiny skull); drab grey-brown, not violet.
- parts:
  1. pellet body: ellipse centre (32,36) rx20 ry14 with bumpy edge, base #6b5a4a (stone family warmed)
  2. fur tufts: 4 short 2px strokes along the edge, #8a7a64
  3. dark patches: two 5x4 ovals at (24,40) and (40,32), #4a3c30
  4. bone rib: 2px curved line (36,28) to (44,22), #f1ece0
  5. skull bit: 4x4 disc at (22,30), #f1ece0, 1px #2a1a10 socket pair
  6. bone tip: 3x2 at (46,40), #f1ece0
- joins: none.
- face: none (skull sockets only, not eyes).
- notes: bone bits are pale, cream-safe, umber outline all round. Silhouette lumpy, not a smooth egg.
