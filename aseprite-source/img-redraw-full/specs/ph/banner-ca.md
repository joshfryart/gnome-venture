# ph/banner-ca
- canvas: 240x80, mode scene, draw at half-size (120x40), write 2x. Full-bleed, no halo, no text.
- subject: Region banner of golden cornfields: warm yellow sky with a low orange sun at right, two rolling tan hills, and rows of green corn stalks with gold cobs on a moss field.
- must-read-at-16px: yellow-gold band with a small orange sun top right and rows of tiny green stalks along the bottom.
- distinct: repeating corn stalks (green stem, two diagonal leaves, oval gold cob) in two sizes, small back row and bigger offset front row; orange sun disc at right.
- parts (final px):
  1. sky: #f5cf6a full canvas, 3 hard bands slightly lighter at top
  2. sun: circle (187,20) r11 #f08a24 with inner ring r7 #f6b85a
  3. far hill: wavy ridge y 36..44, #dcb06a, down to the bottom
  4. near hill: wavy ridge y 46..52, #e8a317
  5. field: rect y 58..80, #b0bc66 (moss #a4b86a)
  6. back corn row: ten stalks spaced 24 px from x 12, stem y 52..64, leaves #7ab55c, cob 3x6 #e8a317
  7. front corn row: eight stalks spaced 34 px from x 17, stem y 58..74, bigger leaves, cob 4x8 #e8a317
- joins: thread-gold hem stitch along the top and bottom edges.
- face: none.
- notes: corner darkening only as a hard band. Cobs gold on green so they pop. Front row overlaps the field with a contact shadow.
