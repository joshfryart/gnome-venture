# ph/creature-cave-glowworm
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A long, low, segmented glowworm crawling left to right whose tail end glows warm yellow like a lantern; gentle and sleepy.
- must-read-at-16px: a horizontal row of tan segments ending in a bright glowing yellow tail ball.
- distinct: five-segment horizontal caterpillar with a glowing tail lamp (cream core in gold) at the right end and a small brown head at the far left; the only creature that carries a light.
- parts (back to front):
  1. glow: two hard rings (no transparency) centred (50,42): outer r13 #f0bf56, inner r9 #f8db8c; stay inside frame
  2. body segments: five circles r5.9 overlapping 2px at (14,47) (21,45.5) (28.5,45.5) (36,47) (43,48.5), tan #dcb06a (wood rim #d9a660)
  3. tail lamp: circle (50,44) r6.3 gold #f5cf6a; core circle (50,43.5) r3.6 cream #f8eed2
  4. head: circle (10.5,45) r4.5 brown #a5663a (wood #a8582f)
  5. segment grooves: 1px #b07a45 gaps between segments
- joins: segment joins read as grooves; optional thread seam along the back.
- face: head is under 20 px, use small eye (3x3 disc, 2x2 pupil) at (9,43); no mouth.
- notes: glow is stepped rings, not a gradient; body low (y40..53) so it reads as a worm; each segment at least 2px visible.
