# landing/roads
- canvas: 240x160, mode scene (full-bleed, no halo), draw at half-size 120x80, write 2x. Hem stitch allowed top and bottom.
- subject: Dusk landscape: glowing mycelium roads (thread-gold dotted paths) linking three mushroom houses, with a small gnome walking; calm and magical.
- must-read-at-16px: dark indigo sky over brown ground with a gold curving dotted road.
- distinct: a curving gold dotted road sweeping lower-left to upper-right with a Y branch, three differently coloured mushroom houses (violet, pink, gold) at different scales, crescent moon top right.
- parts (back to front), final 240x160 coords:
  1. sky: full rect, hard bands of #33305c (about 5 steps, lighter toward the horizon)
  2. stars: 2px #f5cf6a at (30,24), (70,40), (120,20), (160,46), (214,70)
  3. moon: crescent centre (198,29) r12 #f4f0e0, bitten by a sky-colour disc offset right and down
  4. ground: wavy hill from y~118 at left, dipping to 112 centre, 104 right, down to y160, #44281c
  5. road: curve (10,140)->(110,112)->(232,90), 6px faint glow #f5cf6a plus 2px dotted core; branches (110,112)->(66,84) and (110,112)->(140,78)
  6. violet house: cap dome centred (66,86) w~28 h~16 #7a5cc0 with 3 white dots, cream stem 9x13 #f4f0e0
  7. pink house: centred (140,80), largest, cap w~42 #e46a9b, shade band #a8241f, cream stem with glowing gold door #f5cf6a
  8. gold house: centred (206,94), cap w~31 #e8a317, shade #b07a10
  9. gnome: at (36,128), about 40 px tall, blue tunic #3f93b2, cream beard, skin #f6c9a0, red pointed hat #d6322b with cream brim and gold bobble
- joins: gold hem stitch along top and bottom edges optional.
- face: gnome has two small locked eyes (3x3 disc, 2x2 pupil) at about (32,102) and (40,102); no blush.
- notes: gnome is tiny; road glow draws the eye; the three house caps must differ in size and colour.
