# flowers/sunflower
- canvas: 64x64, mode sticker, 1x.
- subject: A top-down sunflower head: ring of long yellow petals around a big brown seed disc; cheerful.
- must-read-at-16px: dark brown disc surrounded by a clear ring of bright yellow petals.
- parts:
  1. back petal ring: ~12-16 long narrow ellipses (7 wide x 20 long) fanning from centre (32,32), tips at radius ~31; base #e8a317 (gold shade)
  2. front petal ring: same count rotated half a step, shorter (tips radius ~28), base #f5cf6a (gold rim)
  3. disc: circle (32,32) r13, base #7a4a1f (soil; edge ring 1-2px #3d2a1c)
  4. seed dots: ~10 single 1px dots #d9a05b scattered in a spiral inside the disc
- joins: none.
- face: none.
- notes: petals must be a clean fan at least 2px wide; use 12+12 if tips mush. Shade side lower-right darker. Petals warm yellow.
