How it works

The wage comes out of your purse now and is held safely. The worker is paid bit by bit as they do the work; anything they don’t earn comes back to you. Jobs stay on the board for 3 days. A one-day job fills their daily slot, longer ones their weekly slot.

They can only water, weed and harvest. Harvests come to you, less a 20% share for them.
