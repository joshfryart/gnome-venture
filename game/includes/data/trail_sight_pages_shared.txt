milestone-sayings|Sights|Sights lore|What the Milestone Says|Scrub the old milestone and it says something new each time. So far: "4½ to Somewhere", "Not far now", "Ask a goat", "You are here (probably)", "Turn back for snacks" and "Onwards, small one".\nNobody carved them. The moss grows back in their shape, and soft moss after [b]rain[/b] is the easiest to scrub.

Total five-leaf clovers: one, under a snail, by you.\nShe comes out to recheck the wet leaves on [b]rainy[/b] days.
