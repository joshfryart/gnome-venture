glimmer-shapes|Sights|The way ahead|Glimmer Shapes|Before the Glowworm Grotto and the Lantern Festival River, the little lights gather into shapes if you count them long enough: a teapot, a very round snail, a kite.\nName the right one at [b]Count the glimmers[/b] and they twinkle twice. The shape changes every day, so come back after dark and try again.
first-stars|Sights|The way ahead|First Stars|The island navigators name the first star of every dusk, and it always points somewhere new.\n[b]Find the first star[/b] works only at dusk. Lore helps, and a Puzzle Pal along is good company for counting. Three stars named and you are a navigator in all but canoe.
gate-riddle|Sights|The way ahead|Wren's Gate Riddle|Every wonder gate hides one line of an old riddle, and Wren Thimblewick has rubbed them all in crayon.\nRead in order, they rhyme: [i]steps that climb and roots that bind, water falling, salt and shine, amber, maze, a goat, a leg, and stones that hum it end to end.[/i]\nNobody knows the answer. Wren thinks that is the point.
book-of-hushes|Sights|The way ahead|The Book of Hushes|Sorrel Inkcap says every hush is a place about to speak. Her book holds what eight of them nearly said: a shell's gossip, a rhyme from the wind, a panda's yawn, a rumble of hooves, a star, a breath of cedar, a gentle sigh and a stork's wingbeat.\nThe bound [b]Book of Hushes[/b] can go on a shelf in your yard, where it makes everything a little calmer.
big-friends-lullaby|Sights|The way ahead|The Big Friends Lullaby|Lyra Fiddlewing wrote a lullaby for the sleepy giants of the lairs, one note from each: a toad's snore, a crab's click-clack, a moth's rustle, a jelly's glow, a web's hum, a scarab's roll, and three sets of footprints.\nLairs are safer approached on tiptoe: choose [b]Sneak[/b] on the Trail and you meet fewer fights, and the threshold Sights pay a little more.
echo-map|Sights|The way ahead|The Echo Map|Tumble Waymarsh maps delves by ear. A tide line, a cool draught, a dropped pebble and a shouted hello each told him something about the first room inside.\nHe was already in there when you shouted, of course. Tumble is always already in there.
lamplighters-round|Sights|The way ahead|The Lamplighter's Round|Somebody put up lanterns before five deep delves and then forgot them. Now they are lit again: one by a lantern, one by a floated candle, one by a borrowed firefly, one by tidy wicks and one by shooing the gulls.\nBurdock Deepdelve says a delve with a light at the door is half as dark inside. He may be exaggerating. Bring your own lantern anyway.
