book.ch2|THE WEEPING CLAY
Below the topsoil lies the clay, cold and grey-brown and very patient. Clay holds water, and it holds on to anything that falls in.
Now the clay is weeping. Something has plugged the old wells, and the water has nowhere to go but out through the walls.
Gnomes call the first part of the clay the Seep Steps, then the Tin Lamp, the Drowned Library and Potter’s Row.
book.claybound|THE CLAYBOUND
Crawlers, crayfish, toads and book-nibbling silverfish, each wearing a crust of grey-brown clay like a coat that does not come off.
They are not wicked. They are stuck, and cross about it. Cure the blight under the clay and the crust crumbles away.
book.silverfin|SILVERFIN THE PAGE-EATER
A silverfish grown as long as a gnome, silver and shy. The blight made her hungry for words, and she ate the Library’s shelves one page at a time.
Cured, she is gentle. She still likes the smell of old paper, and now she is allowed to sniff it.
