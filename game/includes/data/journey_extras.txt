# What the side quests of Wren's Journey v2 give the first time (2026-11-28; claude/proposal-story-2-quests.md 4.2 and 5.2). Named by the
# `extra` field of journey_errands.txt and stop_threads_v2.txt (unlock=place:slug, theme:, recipe:, tea:, shop:, page=slug) and by carried
# items in their steps. Data for journey_v2_open, read by includes/quest_data.php (journey_extras()): nothing here exists in the game while
# the setting is off, and nothing is imported into recipes, teas, landmarks or stop themes until the errands engine grants it.
# Each row keeps the columns of the file it will be imported into, so the import is a copy. No pipe inside a field.
#
# PLACE  | slug | region | zone # | type (haven or wonder) | Gnomish name | flavour                    (landmarks.txt + slug; hidden until unlocked)
# THEME  | variant slug | stop types | tier | name | icon | what it gives everybody | plaque line       (stop_tier_themes.txt; needs = the default
#                                                                                                        row's: a look and words, never a new power)
# RECIPE | slug | Dish name | ingredients | meal buff | buff minutes | energy | description | tier | cook minutes      (recipes.txt)
# TEA    | slug | Name | components | note                                                              (teas.txt)
# SHOP   | slug | region | goods name | what the tier 2 stalls of the area say about it                 (one more line of goods at the area's stalls)
# PAGE   | slug | title | text                                                                          (an Almanac page; counts for the Standing "pages" row)
# ITEM   | slug | Name | what it is                                                                     (carried for an errand, never sold or kept)
# Counts (lint): 13 places, 16 themes (the 12 of the quests doc + 4 that stand in for the dropped Puzzle Pal extras), 13 recipes (the 14th,
# three-sisters-stew, is in recipes.txt already), 4 teas, 11 stall lines.
