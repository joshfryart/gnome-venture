# STAT PIECES (behind the admin setting pfight_statpieces): a gnome OWNS every piece whose tier its points in that stat reach (tier 0 = everyone; points = the stat score, 10 at the start, at most stat_max)
# and packs the ones it wants (off until switched on, like the knacks). Read by pfd_stat_pieces() in includes/pfdeck.php and pfb_stat_data() in includes/pfboard.php; mirrored in tools/pfengine.js (PFB_STAT_ROWS).
# key|name|stat|tier|type|v|vd|edges|tag|ab|live|fx|text
#   stat: nimble lore luck sense.  v / vd: the base number is v + points / vd (vd 0 = no points part); the stat's modifier, level / 3 and the rank bonus are added as for every piece (the stat override: the piece's own stat, not PF_STAT).
#   edges: knob family / column family.  tag: an engine tag (PF_TAGS) or -.  ab: which of the numbers a = 1 + points / 5 and b = ceil(a / 2) the piece uses (a, b, ab, -).
#   live: 1 = its fx clauses run in a fight (the tile carries fx S:KEY); 0 = loaded but inert (no fx on the tile) until its own slice builds and tests it.
#   fx: wear-effect clauses (pfb_wx_*, data/wear_fx.txt), ' ; ' between clauses; the condition roll / !roll = the shared Luck roll (50% + 2% per Luck point, at most 90%, once a firing).  text: {a} {b} = the numbers.
#   stat-piece actions (PFB_STAT_ACTS in pfboard.php): again[:link] = fire once more (itself / the piece of yours it links to), fup:N = the creature's next Hurt +N, pinch:hi|lo = take its strongest | weakest hand piece into your next hand,
#   coin:back|cut:N = its strongest hand piece back to its draw pile | -N, streak:N = roll until a miss (at most N), each hit refires a random piece of yours; pluck:roll = Pluck with the Luck roll (halved on a boss).
#   Nimble: shove = the creature's aim moves to the free cell next to it farthest from its pieces (once a round; no fit = it picks again, not on its old cell), stance = while it stands a creature re-pick may only use a cell touching it
#   (none = it loses that move), shadow = when the creature places its aimed piece this Keeper jumps from your hand beside it and fires alone (once a fight), phantom:N = for the next N rounds you pick (free move p,x,y) the cell next to its aim
#   where its aimed piece lands (once a fight); Skitter's @miss kill on a boss costs its piece one firing instead.
