P|Under every road in the Hollow runs the Waythread, one old fungus that glows where a lamp is lit and boots go by. Once a year the Roundwalkers walked the whole ring of roads. They lit the lamps, mended the benches, carried post and seed, and wrote it down in the Almanac.\n\nThen each walker thought somebody else had the next stretch. Nobody ended the Round. It only became nobody's job. Folk call the years since the Long Quiet.\n\nWren is a bookbinder with a handcart of pages. She wants an Almanac that is true today. Light a stop, plant its bed, come back often, and she will follow one area behind you, on the lit road, with a letter.
