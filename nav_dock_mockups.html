Gnome Venture dock

Version 2. The dock is a garden hedge with jigsaw pieces locked along the curve. Four menu pieces, two action pieces, the status piece in the middle. Sub-menus are strips of interlocking pieces that slide up. Try Garden: left piece is Select (your multi-select panel), right piece is the tool you cycle with.

How it works

Puzzle pieces, garden dock

The dock is soil with a grass and flower edge. Pieces lock together along the curve, knob into socket, and tilt with the slope. Slips are strips of pieces that click together too. Menu pieces keep the area hues (purple, moss, water, petal). Gold means open or primary.

Four menus, same groups

You and Hobbies on the left, Out & about and Together on the right. Tap one: it lifts and turns gold, a strip slides up. Your current page's piece is gold with "you are here". A gold bar on the menu piece marks the section you are in while the strip is closed.

Left action: Select (Garden)

Garden's left piece is your existing "select multiple" panel. Tap Select: squares become pickable, a Pick strip (Empty, Improver, Tilling, Thirsty, Ready, None, with counts) snaps in on the left and a Then strip (Plant…, Work in…, Mulch…, Sheet mulch…, Till, Water, Harvest, Dig up) on the right. The left piece becomes Done, the right piece counts what is picked, menus lock. Crafting keeps a small All (Pots, Jars, Both).

Left action: Quick slot (Satchel)

The player decides what this button does. Empty: tap, pick a food or potion in the satchel, tap Confirm. Filled: tap uses it, a small red ✕ at the bottom left clears it, and holding the piece clears it on phones. Saved per gnome.

Left action in places: Do here

In a town, stop, haven, delve, lair or wonder the left piece opens what can be done right now. Towns have many tiny interactions, so the dock offers 3 per visit (quest beats first, then a rotating pick, ticks for the ones done) plus More for the full list on the page. The graphical map stays.

On the trail

Left is your Quick item from the Satchel, read-only. Right opens Trail: Sights, Wander, Gather, Sneak, Hunt, Turn back. In an encounter it becomes Fight on and Flee.

Shops

The right piece is a Buy | Sell switch: tap flips the mode. Hold it, or tap the little arrow, for a strip of Single and Several. Each opens its fold on the page and closes the other. The left piece does the deal for what is picked.

Pieces snap together

A strip builds one piece at a time, each sliding into the socket of the one before, with a small overshoot and click. Closing plays it backwards, last piece first. Tap a different menu while one is open: the old strip comes apart while the new one snaps together, at the same moment.

Right action: one at a time

On Garden the right piece is the active tool. Tap cycles to the next tool that has something to do (skips empty ones). Hold, or press the little arrow on top, to open the strip and pick directly. Keyboard: Enter cycles, Arrow Up opens.

Center plate and popover

The plate shows energy, level, time and pals' locations. Tap it: a piece pops up out of its socket with "Right now" numbers and "Jump to" links. See the table below for what is in it and what was left out.

Play mode

Games and fights need the screen. The dock drops to a thin rail with Quit and the main verb. Menus lock until the round ends.

What goes in the center popover

Rule: a link goes in only if it reaches something the menu cannot. If a tile already exists, the popover shows the number as plain text, no link. Almanac came out because You already has it.

Edge cases

Roll-out

1. Behind a flag

New setting nav_dock, admin preview first, same pattern as gnav_on. Landscape only. Portrait keeps today's header, strip and bottom bar.

  • nav_sheet() feeds the slips (same groups, badges, unlocks)
  • status_strip() feeds the plate
  • new dock_actions() registry

2. Pages declare actions

Each page sets $GLOBALS['gv_dock'] with left, right and mode. AJAX pages return it in the swapped fragment so site.js updates the buttons in place.

  • start with Garden, Tasks, World, Satchel
  • then play-mode pages
  • default Back and Hovel for the rest

3. Retire the old chrome

Remove header, status strip, bottom bar, side menu and right rail for flagged players. Move theme, log out, feedback and staff tools to You, Account. Add "turn your phone" hint in portrait.

4. Lock landscape

When portrait usage is low, set "orientation": "landscape" in manifest.json for installed play. Keep portrait fallback for login, register and account in browsers.
