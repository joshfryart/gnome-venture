Route Map Layout

Every region map with its roads, and every route drawn tile by tile. "Today" is the flat 22×7 strip that always plays left to right. "New design" is the 30×18 sections with a road that wanders and leaves each section on the side the region road points to, joined end to end.

Same tile size on both views

Today a section is 22×7 tiles and the screen shows all 7 rows. The new section is 30×18 tiles, so the camera scrolls up and down as well as along. Both views draw a tile at the same size, so the difference in area is real.

How the new road is made

Each section takes its heading from the region road under it, snapped to one of 8 compass directions. The road wanders: never more than 6 tiles straight (one run per section may reach 10). Thickets close in on the inside of the bends, so cutting across the meadow saves at most about a fifth of the walk.

Joins and stops

The exit tile of one section sits against the entry tile of the next, on the same row or column. Grey squares are stop placeholders. Landmarks use each route's own names; finds sit out in the landscape, away from the road.
