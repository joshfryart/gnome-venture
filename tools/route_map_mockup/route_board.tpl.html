What the sizes mean

Now: 22 × 7 tiles a section, 2 to 4 sections a route, and the screen always shows the full 7 rows. Mockup: about 30 × 18 tiles a section (red), so the camera scrolls up and down as well as along.

What "follows the path" means

Each section is turned to the heading of the region-map road under it. The first section starts at the stop it leaves and the last one ends at the stop it reaches, so a road heading north-east plays north-east.
