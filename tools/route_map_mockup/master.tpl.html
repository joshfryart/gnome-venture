All 14 regions on the world map with every route in its new design: 30×18 sections joined end to end, the road wandering inside them. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, or pick a region to jump to it. Zoom in far enough and each section's tiles appear.

Each route is scaled so its first and last tile span the same distance as the road on the region map, and centred on it. Sections keep their own headings, so a route that snaps to 8 directions can drift a little off the drawn road. At hubs, routes that leave in a fan overlap.
