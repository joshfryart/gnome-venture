Puzzle Combat: playable demo

A fight from Gnome Venture, using the real rules. Pick a creature, play a few turns, and tell me what is confusing or fun.

How a fight works
  1. The creature always has pieces on the board. Pick a piece from your hand, then tap a glowing spot to place it. Edges must fit: a knob goes into a hole of the same shape, and a flat edge fits anything.
  2. When you place a piece, its chain scores, newest piece first, through every joint where a knob sits in a hole (the small stitch mark). A flat contact breaks the chain.
  3. Fill the bar at the top to win. Hurt pieces (orange) fill it from the left and make the creature back off. Charm pieces (pink) fill it from the right and win it over for a better reward. Guard and heal keep you alive meanwhile.
  4. To build a bigger play, pick a piece and choose "set aside with" another to join them. Next turn add one more (three at most), then play the group for 25% more.
  5. Tap any piece on the board to read about it. Tap a history chip at the top to rewind the board to that moment.
One piece a turn. The creature answers after End turn.

Demo built from the game's own code and art. Edge cases may differ slightly from the live game.
