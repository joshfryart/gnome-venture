How a rig is put together

The sheet of the foe on the stage. Top row: each part, painted whole, even where another part covers it. Bottom row: its outline. The engine draws all outlines behind all paint, so a creature keeps one clean outline however its parts move.

What changed for the gnome

  • New bones: head, beard, scarf, eyelid, and elbows on both arms. Before, head, beard and body were one piece and the arm was a stick.
  • Beard and scarf are springs, like the hat. They trail every lunge and settle afterwards with no extra animation written.
  • The head moves a beat after the body and the forearm a beat after the upper arm, so swings whip.
  • After a Pluck or an Attack he hops back and lands with a squash. He used to slide.
  • He blinks, breathes, and has five fidgets.
  • Guard is now forearms up with the chin tucked. Snack is hand to mouth with three chews.
