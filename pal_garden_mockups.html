Rules in these mockups

  • When: the work happens at the moment you sleep, in a fixed order: pests, weeds, harvest, plant, water, then crops grow. Sleep several days in a row and it runs each day in turn.
  • Time modes: 1/3/7 sleeps, or full time until recalled. Counting starts at your next sleep.
  • Tiredness: 100% for 7 days on the same slot and garden, falling to 60% on day 14, then 50%. A ! and a note appear on day 14. A different job or a home rest day resets it. numbers to tune
  • Plant: same seed as before; or the seeds you ticked in the grid. Never rare seeds, seeds a job/order needs, or out-of-season seeds. Squares marked hands off, trees and cover crops are skipped.
  • XP: pals earn reduced Gardening XP from these jobs (suggest 40% of today's) and a daily cap, so big gardens don't level them instantly.
  • Hired helpers: retired, active contracts refunded pro rata, player told once.
