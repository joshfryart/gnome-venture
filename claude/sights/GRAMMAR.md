# Trail Sights: data grammar (reference for the data files). Engine: `game/includes/trail_sights.php`, `trail_sight_extras.php`. Lint: `bash tests/trail_sights_lint.sh` (set `SIGHTS_LINT_DIR=/tmp/sights-lint-<you>` so you have your own scratch install).

All files are in `game/includes/data/`. Each kind has a base file and one file per SET (`maplewold`, `shared`, `end`); an agent edits only its own set's files. Lines starting `#` and blank lines are ignored. No `|` or newline inside a field.

## Sight rows: `trail_sights_<set>.txt` (13 columns)
`slug|verb|icon|energy|cd|uses|when|check|rewards|win|lose|tool|fail_en`
- verb: unique across ALL rows (lint). icon: one emoji. energy 0-5. cd = cooldown in tenths of a game day (55 = 5.5). uses per cooldown (1-9).
- `when`: `-` or conditions joined by `/`; each is `key=v1,v2` (any one value). Keys: `dp` (dawn day dusk night), `se` (spring summer autumn winter), `we` (sun cloud rain storm frost heat wind fog snow), `hp` (a stop happening id), **`st`** (the walk stance: `sneak`, `hunt`, `normal`), **`pal`** (`1` = a Puzzle Pal is along, `0` = alone), **`tl=<key>:N`** (the private tally `<key>` is at least N).
- check: `-` | `stat:dc` (stat keys: grit nimble hardiness lore sense charm luck ... see GNOME_STATS in `game/includes/gnome.php`; dc 8-14) | **`pick:N`** or **`pick:N:label;label;label`** (N 2-5 labels: the gnome chooses one; one is right, fixed per gnome and game day; wrong = the lose line; a pick needs a lose line). A fail costs `fail_en` extra energy.
- tool: `-` or `type:tier` (types: forage pick net bucket axe rod basket staff lantern; tier 1-4).
- win / lose: one line each; **variants joined by ` // `** (a random one shows). No emoji inside (the icon column shows one).
- rewards: comma-joined tokens. Each token may have `@chance` (percent) and `?cond` (conditions as in `when`, with `;` instead of `,` between values, `/` between conditions: `item=x:1@25?se=summer;autumn/dp=dusk`), and `first:` in front = only the first time this gnome ever uses the Sight.
  Tokens: `xp=3:1` `c=4:1` (coins) `en=3` `item=<slug>:<qty>` `crop=1` `find=1` `fish=1` `seed=1` `buff=<stat>:<amount>:<minutes>` `fp=1` (friendship with a RANDOM villager of the start place) `reveal` (tells a hidden place) `gather=<tool>` (the ordinary gather table) `take=<item>:<qty>` (a cost: needed and used up on a win) and the new ones:
  - `hint=weather|season|away|creature`: a TRUE line from the game state (tomorrow's weather, the season, who is away today, a large creature nearby).
  - `title=<Name>`: a title the gnome can wear (shown on the Almanac page's title list).
  - `page=<key>`: opens an extra Almanac page defined in `trail_sight_pages_<set>.txt`.
  - `tally=<key>`: adds 1 to a private counter (a cairn, a collection); then `...?tl=<key>:5` tokens pay at milestones.
  - `fpto=<Name>[@village-slug][:points]`: friendship with a NAMED villager (default 1 point).
  Item slugs must exist (the lint checks the DB, the items files and the new items files).

## Villagers at a Sight: `trail_sight_folk_<set>.txt`
`sight slug|villager|when|chance %|line|rewards`
- villager = an exact name (a stop villager or a town NPC/traveller); when two villagers share a name use `Name@village-slug` (the village's location slug, e.g. `v-na-1-3`).
- Chance is rolled once per gnome, Sight, villager and game day (so the Sights card can say who is here). A villager who is **away today** (visiting family) or a **hidden NPC** (Pomeroy Crumbworth before the Gourmand opens) is not there.
- **Default hours by role** (2026-11-14, Josh): a row whose `when` has no `dp=` is NOT there all day; it follows the villager's role (`trail_sight_folk_role_hours`): a travelling NPC (merchant, minstrel, naturalist, the Gourmand) follows their circuit: on the Trail of a region (the walk's start or end region) from 3 stops before they reach it to 4 stops after (8 of the 14 twelve-hour stops: about 4 days a week), any time of day; a town NPC with a schedule is out unless the schedule has them at home; a stop villager whose role is Innkeeper, Keeper, Watcher, Lighthouse keeper, Signaller, Moth-watcher, Lair-watcher, Well-dweller or Scarecrow: any time; Pedlar, Pilgrim, Wanderer, Scout, Porter, Sack-carrier: road hours (dawn, day); every other role (a stall, a trade, a shop): in the day only (Josh 2026-11-14). Write `dp=` in the row to override.
- rewards: any Sight reward tokens, plus: **`nocheck`** (their being here makes the Sight's check a sure thing) and **`give=<item>:<qty>`** (they ask for that item: the gnome gets a "Give" button on the Sights card; the item is used up and the OTHER tokens pay). `fp=N` = friendship with THIS villager. A row with `give=` pays only on giving; a row without pays automatically after a win (the first present one).

## Quest lines: `trail_sight_quests_<set>.txt`
`Q|slug|title|giver|icon|order|reward|intro|outro|gate` then `B|slug|sight slug or @kind|label|when|needs`
- order: `seq` (in order), `any`, **`anylast`** (any order but the LAST beat only after all the others).
- gate (optional): `quest:<other quest slug>` = cannot begin until that line is done.
- A Sight may be a beat more than once in a line. Beat kinds: a Sight slug, or **`@boss`** (a lair is won: defeated or befriended; `@boss:<lair slug>` for one lair), **`@meal`** (a home-cooked meal eaten), **`@friend:<Name[@village]>`** (that villager is at least an acquaintance).
- needs (beat column 6, optional): `take=<item>:<qty>`: the beat counts only if the gnome carries it; it is used up.
- giver: a villager's name (`Name@village-slug` if shared). reward as the rewards column (`fp=N` = friendship with the giver; `title=`, `page=` allowed).

## New items: `trail_sight_items_<set>.txt`
`slug|Name|category|rarity|base_price|edible|description|effects` (effects optional JSON). categories: forage part decor food produce supply other misc flower soil. Decor you can place in a yard: edible 0, effects `{"decor":{"type":"ornament","w":1,"h":1,"icon":"🎐","appeal":4,"bonus":""}}` (look at `game/includes/animals120.php` ~line 56 for types). Something to eat or use that gives a short buff: edible 1, effects `{"temp":{"charm":1,"minutes":120}}` (stats: see `game/includes/alchemy.php` ALCHEMY_ITEMS for examples). The picture is a 64x64 PNG `game/assets/img/ph/<slug>.png` (art is made by someone else; list new item slugs in your todo file).

## Extra Almanac pages: `trail_sight_pages_<set>.txt`
`key|Chapter|Category|Title|Body` (Body uses `\n` for a line break and the Almanac's `[b]bold[/b]`); opened by a `page=<key>` reward. Use chapter `Sights` (or `General`).

## New villagers: `trail_sight_newfolk_<set>.txt`
`Name|species|personality|role|bio|village slug`: a villager the world did not make (Keeper Marram ...), added to that village's stop villagers (slot 9+). Then rows may name them. (Find village slugs: location slugs like `v-na-1-3`; list them with a scratch install, see `claude/sights/folk-todo.md` for the snippet.)

## Names and captions: `trail_sight_info_<set>.txt` (`slug|Name|caption`): one per Sight; already written.


## 2.7.00 additions (engine gaps; tests `tests/trail_sights_270.sh`)
- **`gd=M:R`** (a `when` / `?cond` key): every Mth game day, i.e. the gnome's game day mod M = R. Several values = any of them: `gd=7:0,7:3`. With the game clock off it counts real days. Example: a villager on every 7th game day: `when` = `gd=7:0`.
- **`choose=A;B;C`** (a reward token, 2 to 4 plain rewards joined by `;`, no `pick:` check on the row): the gnome picks ONE reward on the Sights card before using the Sight (one button per option; refused without a choice). Options: `c= xp= en= item= crop= seed= fish= find= buff= disc=`.
- **`disc=rest|shop:percent:minutes`**: a temporary discount (real minutes, max 60%): `rest` = a room at the inn, `shop` = shop prices. A smaller one never replaces a bigger one that still runs. Stored in `users.ev_disc`.
- **Tool-tier odds** `item=x:1@5/15/30/50`: the chance by the tier of the row's `tool` (tier 1 = first number ... a tier past the list repeats the last). The row needs a tool column.
- **`check=stat:dc`** (a reward token on a VILLAGER row, `trail_sight_folk_*.txt`): the villager only pays if the gnome passes that stat check; otherwise their line says they ask for more.
- Rewards pass: `trail_sight_flat_scale` (setting `sights_flat_pct_per_level`, 0 = off): see `claude/sights-rewards-2.7.00.md`.

- 2.7.06: `check` may be either / or: `nimble:9+grit:11` (any one passing wins; the card says "Nimble or Grit"). `@chance` also works on `hint=` `title=` `page=` `fpto=` `disc=`.
- 2.7.06: **streaks**: reward token `streak=<key>` counts consecutive game days the Sight (or any Sight with that key) is used; a missed game day starts again at 1; the same day twice changes nothing. Condition `sk=<key>:N` = a streak of at least N days (checked after this use's bump, so it pays on the use that reaches N).
- 2.7.06: **loans**: `lend=item:qty` hands an item over on the first use (needs it in the satchel); the next use of the same Sight pays `repay=item:qty` (usually more) and the loan is closed; then it can be lent again. Stored as the tally `lend#<sight slug>`.
- 2.7.06: `buff=stat:amount:sleep` lasts until the gnome's next sleep (their game day moves on; users.ev_day), instead of a number of minutes. Needs the sleep-days clock; without it the buff falls back to 60 minutes.
- 2.7.06: **quest time limit**: an optional 11th column on a `Q|...` row (after the gate, which may be empty) = game days to finish the line from its first beat. Past it the line times out (progress dropped) and starts again at the next first beat. Stored as the tally `qstart#<quest slug>`.
- 2.7.06: `take=any-<kind>:N` = any N items of a kind: the produce of every plant of that kind (any-nut, any-fruit, any-vegetable ...), else every item whose slug ends with it (any-feather). Different kinds of item may be mixed; the biggest stacks go first.
- 2.7.06: condition `fd=yes` (any festival is on today) or `fd=<festival slug>[,..]` (that festival). **Villager stat check** was already there: a `check=stat:dc` token in a folk row's rewards (2.7.01); a miss pays none of that row's other rewards.
- 2.7.06: reward `time=N` = N more game minutes pass after a win (1-480; a long sit-down, a slow walk). Needs the sleep-days clock.
- 2.7.06: `buff=stat:amount:delve` is a lucky moment that only counts while a delve or lair run is active (users.ev_scope = 'delve'); it stays until replaced (30 real days at most).
- 2.7.06: `buff=stat:amount:section` is a lucky moment that lasts until the Trail walk crosses into the next section (users.ev_scope = 'section', ev_day = expedition id * 1000 + section); without a Trail walk it falls back to the usual minutes.
- 2.7.06: **escorts**: reward `escort=Name` (a stop villager with a picture) makes that villager walk with you for the rest of this Trail walk: they are drawn beside the gnome on the map (both the baked-art and the plain tile renderers; state `escort` in the walk scene). Ends with the walk. Checked by eye: first frame of the 64x80 strip, left of the gnome.

## 2.7.08 additions (tests `tests/trail_sights_278.sh`)
- **`to=<place slug|kind|land>[,..]`** (a `when` / `?cond` key): the walk leads there. The place slug is the destination's location slug (`lm-ea-2-wonder-panda-bamboo-grove`), the kind is `haven wonder delve lair village ...`, the land is the destination's landscape. The lint checks each value against the world.
- **`{cond}` line variants**: a `win` / `lose` variant may START with `{cond}` (the `when` grammar, e.g. `{to=lm-ca-4-lair-smokecap-caldera}`, `{we=fog}`, `{dp=night/se=winter}`). A matching `{cond}` variant WINS over the plain ones; with none matching a plain one shows. Keep at least one plain variant. No other `{ }` in a line.
- **`mk=yes|no`**: the town market day (`market_day_on()`, setting `market_day`).
- **`hint=tide`** (the true tide now: "low tide now, turning in 3 h") and **`hint=moon`** (tonight's phase, and big / small tides).
- **`take=` may carry a `?cond`** (`take=sardine:1?ti=low`): it is only needed and used up when the condition holds. Still no `first:` or `@chance` on a take.
- **Per-walk cap**: setting `sights_walk_cap` (default 6, 0 = off): at most that many Sight uses (win or lose) on one Trail walk; the card shows "6 Sights this walk: the most". Gifts (`give=`) do not count.

## 2.7.08 additions, second pass (tests `tests/trail_sights_279.sh`)
- **`buff=all:N[:minutes|sleep|delve|section]`**: +N to EVERY stat (one lucky moment; it replaces any other lucky moment, as every `buff=` does). The lint now checks every `buff=`: `stat:amount[:length]`, stat one of GNOME_STATS or `all`.
- **`fpall=N`** (1-9): N friendship points with EVERY stop villager of the place the walk began at (the picnic "the whole town likes you"). `fp=` stays one random villager; `fpto=` one named villager.
- **A free pick** (a reward choice inside a pick): a `pick:N:label;label;...` row with a `choose=A;B;...` of exactly N options. Every label is right (no wrong answer, no lose line used) and label i pays option i. The card shows one button per label with its reward ("dragon: 5 XP"). A `choose=` on a pick with another number of options is refused by the lint.
- **Quest order `firstlast`** (`Q|...|firstlast|...`): the first beat must come first, the last beat last, the beats between in any order (Q4 / Q5 "start at X").
- **A beat's gift**: an optional 7th column on a `B|` row: reward tokens paid when that beat counts (`B|line|314-owlrock|the owl's letter|-||item=owl-letter:1`; `fp=N` = friendship with the giver). No costs or choices (`take= choose= give= tally= ...` are refused). The `needs` column (6th) may be empty.
- **`qd=<quest slug>[,..]`** (a `when` / `?cond` / `{cond}` key, built earlier, documented now): one of those Sight quest lines is done (after-quest effects: the meadow forecast after KITE, Marrowby's stall ...).
- Still NOT possible (2.7.08): see `claude/trail-2.7.08-notes.md` "Second pass".

## 2.7.08 third pass A (tests `tests/trail_sights_280.sh`; engine `game/includes/trail_sights_3a.php`)
- **`buff2=stat:amount[:minutes|sleep|delve|section]`**: a SECOND lucky moment (its own slot, `users.ev2_*`), so one row can give two buffs: `buff=sense:1:60,buff2=luck:1:60` (314-lanternrocks). `buff=` still replaces only slot 1, `buff2=` only slot 2; `stat` may be `all`. Both show on the Today / status effects list ("A lucky moment", "A second lucky moment") and each runs out by its own length. The lint refuses two unconditional `buff=` in one row (the second would just replace the first: write it as `buff2=`); two `buff=` with different `?cond` or `@chance` are fine.
- **`hint=snore|webhum|glow|rut`**: a true line about the lair AHEAD (the walk's destination when it is a lair, else the first lair of the start place's region): how many nights since this gnome last went into it (its lair runs, kept for good). Never = "slept undisturbed by you"; 0 = "wide awake"; 1-2 dozing, 3-6 sound asleep, 7+ deep asleep. Same fact, four wordings: snores counted, the web humming low / high, the glow pulsing slow / fast, the rut's age.
- **`hint=firstroom`**: the first rooms of the delve AHEAD (destination, else the region's delve): the delve route is seeded per gnome, delve and day, so "today it opens on a fight, a treasure room or a puzzle room" is true. It also leaves a note: when the gnome goes into that delve, the delve's log says it again from the run's real route ("Just as the Sight told you ...", or "the rooms have shifted since" on another day). Needs the v2 delves (setting `delve_v2`); without them it says nothing.
- **`nxt=<effect>`**: an effect on the NEXT stretch of this Trail walk, kept in the walk state and used up once when the walk goes on into a new stretch (walking back keeps it; a stretch already walked keeps it for the next new one). `find[:1-3]` (that many extra finds on it), `calm` (no surprise at its door), `quick:5-60` (that many game minutes back: arrive sooner), `skip` (a shortcut: the stretch starts at its far end, ready to go on, its walking time back), `peek` (told at once: how many finds the next stretch has). On the last stretch it says "No stretch is left on this walk for that". `@chance` and `?cond` work. Game time needs the sleep-days clock.
- The 2.7.06 escort now walks on into the next stretch (it was dropped at the first Continue).

## 2.7.08 third pass B (tests `tests/trail_sights_281.sh`; engine `game/includes/trail_sight_third_b.php`; no schema change)

Read on the gnome's OWN game day (sleep-days clock: game_day -> date -> `hollow_season` / `hollow_season_day`); with the clock off, today. Lint: a known villager, a real season, day 1-120, one row a villager.
  - **`bd=<name-slug>[,..]|any`** (a `when` / `?cond` / `{cond}` key): it is that villager's birthday today (`bd=lyra-fiddlewing`; the slug is the name in lower case with `-`, without `@village`); `bd=any` = anyone on the list. ## Fourth pass (2026-11-19, not released yet; tests `tests/trail_sights_282.sh`; engine `game/includes/trail_sights_4.php`; no schema change)
- **Fog: `nxt=fog`** (one more `nxt=` effect): the NEXT stretch lies in fog. Its finds are not drawn on the map (and have no "Something to find" button) until the gnome stands on or next to one; they are still there and are picked up by walking onto them. `nxt=peek` cannot see into a foggy stretch ("you cannot tell what waits there"). The fog stays on that stretch for the walk (walking back and forth keeps it); the map legend says "🌫️ Fog: finds show when you are next to them". Fights, Sights, villagers and the way on always show.
- **A lit path: `nxt=lit`** (the Lantern Path): from now to the end of this walk no stretch is in fog: a fog that waits is dropped, the fog on the stretch you stand in lifts, a later `nxt=fog` says "your lit path keeps it off". Works on the last stretch too. In the data: `rainforest-the-lantern-fruit` `nxt=lit?dp=night`.
- **Foggy weather**: setting `trail_fog_weather` (Admin settings, default OFF). When on, every stretch a gnome newly enters while the weather is fog is in fog (the first one too), unless the path is lit. Off = only Sights make fog.
- **A named stretch: `sec=<route id>.<stretch>:<effect>`**: an effect aimed at one stretch of one route instead of "the next one". `<stretch>` is 1-6 as the route is drawn (stretch 1 is at the route's a end; a walk from the b end meets them in the opposite order). Effects: `find[:1-3]`, `calm`, `fog`, `quick:5-60`, `skip` (as in `nxt=`; no `peek`, no `lit`). It is kept for the gnome (private tally `sec#route#stretch#effect`) until one of their walks ENTERS that stretch, on this walk or any later one, then it is used up; the Sight's line names it: "A find waits on stretch 2 of the Trail between A and B". `find` keeps the biggest count, `quick` the most minutes; the same effect twice does not stack. On a walk's FIRST stretch only `find` and `fog` are used (calm / quick / skip wait for a walk that comes into that stretch from another one). A stretch walked before on the same walk (walking back) uses nothing. `@chance` and `?cond` work. Lint: the route exists and has that many stretches. In the data: `307-shellhollow` `sec=314.2:find@50?dp=night`, `316-starstones` `sec=309.2:find?dp=night/se=winter`, `305-birchtunnel` `sec=305.2:find@40` and in fog `sec=312.1:find@40`.
- **Penalties: `lose:` in front of a reward token** = paid when the check (or a pick with a wrong answer) is LOST, never on a win: `lose:time=20` (20 more game minutes pass), `lose:nxt=fog` (or any `nxt=`), `lose:sec=...`. `?cond` and `@chance` work: `lose:time=15?we=wind;storm`. Only `time= nxt= sec=`; the row needs a check or a real pick (not a free pick); not on villager rows; no `first:`. The lose line shows them: "Wrong door. (⏱️ 20 min lost, 🌫️ Fog settles on the next stretch ...)". The `fail_en` column is still the energy penalty.
- **A check that depends on the conditions: `stat:dc?cond:dc2`** = difficulty dc2 while the condition holds, else dc: `sense:11?se=spring:9` (bluebells are easier to hear in spring). The condition is the `when` grammar (`,` between values, `/` between conditions); works inside an either / or (`nimble:9+sense:12?we=fog:14`). The Sights card says "🎲 Sense (easier right now)" / "(harder right now)". In the data: `forest-the-bluebell-glade`.
- **`time=N`** is now linted (1-480 game minutes).
