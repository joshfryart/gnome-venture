# Gnome Venture: listing writeup (draft)

**Gnome Venture** is a free, cosy browser game: tend a garden, cook and craft, make friends with the villagers of the Hollow, and walk the roads of a hand-drawn world of 14 regions. No download. Works on a phone.

## What's new

**Sleep-days: your own game clock.** Energy no longer trickles back by the minute. You get a day (06:00 to 22:00), do things, then sleep: in your hovel, on a bench, at a friend's house, at an inn or on the road with camping gear. A nap gives a little back.
- A clock with the day sits at the top of every page, next to your energy.
- Plants grow while you sleep (water them that day, or let it rain). Jars, cooking pots and drying racks follow your days too. Pals work on game time.
- Shops keep opening hours. Villagers are sometimes away visiting family.
- Food heals you. Gathering takes 10 game minutes and finds 2 or 3 items.
- A new Hovel page shows your clock, the weather and what each kind of rest gives.

**Routes with real maps.** Every road between towns is now a hand-shaped walk (230+ routes, 600+ sections) with its own path, set pieces and finds off the beaten track. Walk it tile by tile, meet creatures on the way, and watch Puzzle Pals follow your gnome.

**2.5D gnome.** Your gnome is drawn with a full animated rig that faces the way it walks (front, side, back), in your own hat, beard and scarf colours. It stands on the Today page, the Trail, delves and fights, and the colour pickers recolour it live.

**Tabbed news.** A News tab for announcements and a Release notes tab for every update.
