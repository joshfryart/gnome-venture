# Art brief: Here pictures, streets, doors, sprites (2026-11-04)
Source of truth for slots: `game/includes/data/here_layouts.json` (edit in Admin → Picture layouts; lint with `php tools/lint_here.php`). Placeholders showing every slot: `python3 tools/gen_here_placeholders.py --out DIR`. Canvas size comes from each layout's `w` x `h` (480x270 today; Josh may change it, so read the JSON).

## Here scenes (`assets/img/scenes/here-<key>.png`)
- Keys: `generic`, `town`, `village`, `stop`, `haven`, `wonder`, `delve`, `lair` now; later `<kind>-<landscape>` (landscapes: coast mountain forest desert rainforest grassland wetland tundra island), `slug:<place-slug>` becomes `here-slug-<place-slug>.png`. Lookup: slug, kind-landscape, kind, generic.
- PNG, exactly the layout w x h, pixel art, no baked-in people or animals (villagers are sprites).
- Horizon at the layout's `horizon` y: everything above is sky and far scenery, below is ground. Ground must be flat and plain around every `stand` point (28+ units clear) so sprites read.
- 8 path stubs (N NE E SE S SW W NW) leaving the picture at the `exits` anchors; each ends in a clear patch where the tap circle (r 26) sits. Leave room: no detail in the circle.
- `spots` are the named things (town: green, teahouse, board, library, shops, homes; village: do, people, garden; haven: fire, shop; delve/lair: mouth; wonder: investigate; `wild`: a wild trail on the right edge). Draw the object centred on the dot.
- Dark theme gets a code tint; no dark variant needed.

## Residential and doors (later release)
- Streets `scenes/resid-<n>.png` (n = 4, 6, 8 house slots), houses by home kind (cottage, burrow, treehouse, hut, stone, boat) with lit and dark window.
- Doors `scenes/door-<homekey>.png` (6 shared, lit and dark) and optional `door-<slug>.png`.

## Villager sprites (`assets/img/sprite/npc-<slug>.png`)
Feet at bottom-centre of the file, 96 px tall at most, transparent background, 1 px umber outline, 2.5D (slight top-down). Missing file falls back to the toy mini, then the bust, then an emoji circle. The scene scales each sprite 0.75x (far) to 1.25x (near) by its feet y.

## Trail tiles
Generated by code from the landscape palette (`tilemap.php`); art is optional. Token icons (32x32) for fight, find and the way on can replace the emoji later.

## Treehouse and Pal Yard (Phase 5; flag `gnav_tree`)
Files: `scenes/tree-house.png` (layout `treehouse`), `scenes/yard-pals.png` (layout `yard`), both w x h from the layout (480x270 today). Red-border placeholders exist (`python3 tools/gen_here_placeholders.py treehouse yard`); real art replaces the same names. Optional layers: `scenes/tree-dress-<k>.png` (k = rug, lights, telescope, banner), pal sprites `assets/img/pals/<pal-key>.png` (feet bottom-centre, 36 px natural; fallback is the sticker, then a crop of the pal's piece, then a silhouette).
Layout schema (`includes/data/here_layouts.json`, same format as `aseprite-source/v3/here-gate/*.layout.json`):
- `spots[{k,x,y,box:[x0,y0,x1,y1]}]`: the painted thing is the button. `box` = the whole room / site (boxes never overlap, never in the compass corner); no `box` = 56x56 around x,y. Treehouse spots: parlour, loft, cellar, puzzles, catfish, contests, books, yard. Yard spots: garden, trip, delve, station, sick, board (= Ledger). Rooms and sites are named in the picture; the tag is added by code, centred, so leave the name out of the art.
- `pals[{x,y}]`: ~24 perches (feet position) in found order; a perch never moves. `party[{x,y}]`: where pals taken along sit by the door. `dress[{at,k,name,x,y,w,h}]`: milestone layers shown at `at` pals (5, 10, 20, 40). `stand`: yard slots for idle pals (at most 5 shown, scaled 0.55 far to 0.95 near by feet y).
- `compass_clear:[x0,y0,x1,y1]` (default bottom-right 100x40): kept empty for the compass and world globe buttons (44 px each).
- A locked room is drawn closed by code (two shutter panels over its box); draw the open room.
- No circles: no round buttons or rings in the art or the UI; a missing image shows nothing extra.

**Status (2026-11-05):** first-pass art is in: `scenes/tree-house.png`, `scenes/yard-pals.png`, `scenes/tree-dress-{rug,lights,telescope,banner}.png`. Sources and scripts: `aseprite-source/v3/tree-yard/` (`treehouse.lua`, `yard.lua`, `dress.lua`, `common.lua`; run with `/tmp/aseq/squashfs-root/AppRun -b --script-param out=<dir>/ --script x.lua`). ## Trophy and animation layers (optional; a missing picture draws nothing; added 2026-11-05)
Both are lists in a layout (`includes/data/here_layouts.json`), drawn above the backdrop and below the creatures, never tappable. `php tools/lint_here.php` checks the entries and the size of any PNG that exists.
- `trophies[{k, kind, at, x, y, w, h}]` (treehouse Contest hall): file `scenes/tree-trophy-<k>.png`, exactly w x h. Shown when the count for `kind` is at least `at`: `expert` = Expert jigsaw trophies held (`ppal_trophies`), `top` = games where the gnome holds this week's top score. Shipped slots: `top-1`, `top-3`, `expert-1`, `expert-3`, `expert-6` (16 x 18, inside the Contest hall box).
- `anim[{k, fx, x, y, w, h, speed?, for?}]` (any layout; `for` = a hotspot id: the layer then plays only while that hotspot has its "!" badge, used by the workshop sections since 2.6.41): file `scenes/<art>-anim-<k>.png` where `<art>` is `tree` (treehouse), `yard`, `craft` (workshop), or `here-<layout key>` for a Here scene (`here-town-anim-<k>.png`). `fx`: `sway` (rotate +-2 deg from the bottom centre, 4 s), `bob` (3 px up, 3 s), `twinkle` (opacity, 2.5 s), `drift` (+-12 px sideways, 12 s), `flicker` (opacity, 1.2 s), `pulse` (scale 1.06, 3 s). `speed`: `slow` (x1.6) or `fast` (x0.6). One static frame per file: the motion is CSS (`.hanim.fx-<name>`), off under `prefers-reduced-motion`, so draw it to look right still. Always shown, decorative (`aria-hidden`).
- Pal sprites: `assets/img/pals/<pal-key>.png` is already read (36 px, feet bottom-centre); without it the sticker, then a crop, then a silhouette.
- Files wanted (none drawn yet): `tree-trophy-top-1`, `tree-trophy-top-3`, `tree-trophy-expert-1`, `tree-trophy-expert-3`, `tree-trophy-expert-6` (16 x 18); anim entries are added to a layout together with the picture (suggested: `tree-anim-lights`, `yard-anim-flag`).

## Gate-view pictures and wander art (2026-11-05, branch ccr-4352abc3-code)
- **Gate view** (`"view":"gate"` in the layout): no exit stubs and no circles. The compass (Routes list, every route named by its destination) and the globe (World map popover) are drawn by the page. Spots carry `box:[x0,y0,x1,y1]` (the hit area, boxes never overlap) and optionally `as:` (the behaviour: town green teahouse board library special1 special2 shops wild homes; stop do people stop wild homes garden; several fronts may share one). Stand slots stay 6 units off every box. Import drawn scenes with `python3 tools/import_gate_layouts.py` (`GATE_ART=<dir>` to read another folder); keys: `town-<region code>` (`town-hub` = Mossbottom), `stop-<type>-<landscape>_<style>` with styles from `includes/data/stop_looks.php`.
- **Wander art** (all optional, a missing file draws nothing): `scenes/wander-<land>.png` banner (9 landscapes, about 480x64), `scenes/wander-deep.png`, `scenes/wander-wild.png` (Hard and Wild tiers), `ico/wander-tier-<gentle|wild|dangerous|deep>.png` (24 px; Medium uses `wild`, Hard and Wild use `dangerous`), `ico/wander-<deeper|roam|back|return|visit>.png` (24 px), `ico/wander-found.png` (24 px).
