# Release notes for players: 2.7.01

Public part first (post on release day). The INTERNAL part waits until `pfight_v1` is on for everyone (Josh: wearables, the Tailor and the new fights stay out of public notes).

## Announcement (ready to paste)

> **Gnome Venture 2.7.01: a street to shop on, villagers with weeks of their own, and a moon.**
> The Shops page now shows Shop Street as a picture, with your town's shops and Mossbottom's side by side. Every villager has a weekly routine, and Trail Sights tell you when someone will be there. The moon and tides change what Sights offer. The Trail map has a new picture layer for every kind of place, and villagers walk on the roads. Full notes below.

## What's new

### Shop Street
- The Shops page (and the Shops tab on the World page) shows one street picture: your town's shops on the near side, Mossbottom's on the terrace.
- Every shop name sits on the picture. Tap a shop to go in.
- A shop that is shut says so, and a shop in another town says "walk to ...".
- The three separate shop lists are now one list under the picture, with your town first.
- 14 regional looks for the street.

### Villagers have weeks
- Every villager now has a routine: a stroll, sitting with neighbours, tending a patch, walking to the next stop, going to market, or visiting a Trail Sight.
- Some villagers never leave their post. Others travel.
- If a shopkeeper is away, a free villager minds the shop ("minding").
- The stop's Villagers tab has a "Right now" list.
- A Sight can tell you when its villager will be there, with a 24 hour strip.

### Moon and tides
- A 28 day month with 8 phases. High and low tides follow the moon: biggest at new and full moon.
- Some Sights only happen at a certain phase or tide.

### The Trail map
- 14 kinds of place (Maplewold, coast, forest, grassland, mountain, desert, rainforest, wetland, island, tundra, haven, wonder, delve, lair) each get their own picture layer, new Sights and a quest line.
- Villagers on a road errand are drawn walking.
- Used-up Sights stay on the map as a faded spyglass.
- 15 new Trail markers, and 11 new looks for Maplewold stops.

### New pictures
- 25 kinds of villager are drawn from three sides, and 11 gnome villagers with them.
- New pictures for 142 Puzzle Pals, creatures and villager portraits.

### Not in this version yet
- Hazard markers on the Trail map, a moon line on Today, and the rest of the Pal pictures.

## INTERNAL: post when `pfight_v1` is on for everyone

### Gentler first fights
- Your first 3 fights have no Thornburst, creature pieces carry no tags, and creature pieces hit softer. A defeat is not a knock-out: the creature wanders off and the walk goes on.
- Your very first fight is always a Garden Snail, with a guide line above the board for the first two fights (the close button turns it off for good).
- A **Hint** button lights the spot a careful player would pick and says why. 3 free hints a fight.
- A red edge and a note on any spot that would cost you health. In your first 5 fights, End turn asks once before such a move.
- After a defeat in your first 5 fights, the result card tells you where the health went and offers **Try again**: the same creature, a new deal, your health as it was.
- A "New today" card the first time you meet full-strength rules.

### 319 creatures
- Every creature now has two or three variants of its own. Each has its own pieces, an ability, a play style, regions and drops. 8 new creature abilities (Drain, Plated, Enrage, Wary, Tough hide, Ambush, Stagger, Regrow).
- Creatures turn up only in their own regions. Weaker small and medium creatures have a smaller bar, so a gnome a few levels under them is not lost.

### Wearables and the Tailor
- Six wearables: Flood Boots, Wayfarer's Hat, Work Gloves, Travel Cloak, Trail Pack, Yarn Scarf. Each is also a puzzle piece for a Keepsake slot; 33 items, three ranks (Common, Lined, Fine), two cuts for most.
- The Tailor (garden level 6, 1,800 Petals, 3 levels) makes and lines them. Twigg's sells the Hat and the Pack; Cat Fish gives the Scarf. Five Sights can give a Common one now and then.
- Guard wearables prick the creature for 2 when played. Your gnome wears what you wear.
- Flood Boots halve the slowdown on a flooded road.

### Fixes
- Evade now stops a poison tick.
- Guard wearables carry Spiky.
- The Rare piece cap counts wearables last.
- Bramble's text says what it does.
