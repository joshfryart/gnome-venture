Trail tiles, pass 2

More realism, still quirky, plus find-spot icons that match the tiles, and the fight rule changed: fights are random encounters; only minibosses and bosses are fixed spots. Scenes use the real Maplewold maps and the real v3 gnome sprite. Red-outlined tiles are placeholders (S start, 9 way on, BOSS fixed fight).

3. Find spots (the search icons), open and searched

These replace the sparkle emoji on the map. Each belongs to its land (same ramps, same outline rule, one quirk, one cream sparkle while unsearched) and has a searched state so a done spot reads at a glance. The in-world sprites sit on a 64x64 tile and sort by their feet row like everything else.

Search icon (UI, 64x64)

A slim wood handle, a gold rim lit from the upper left, a pale glass core with a specular streak, and a little leaf seen through the glass (the quirk). Used for the Gather / Look closer button and the map legend.

4. Random encounters, bosses fixed

Rule. Fights are not markers on the map. They are rolled while the gnome walks. Only a miniboss (delve routes) or a boss (lair routes) is a fixed E on the last section, where the player can see it coming.

PieceProposal
Map dataE now means a fixed miniboss or boss spot, and appears only on the last section of a delve or lair route. Every other E in the authored maps is removed (done for The Maplewold: 313 Beaverdam Tunnels has a miniboss spot, 315 Den of the Great White Owl has a boss spot, all other routes have none). F finds are unchanged.
RollOne roll per tile entered along the walked path, not per tap. Suggested chance: 2% on floor, 8% on rough floor, so tall grass, fern and pebbles are where things pop out, like the art already suggests. No roll in the first 3 tiles of a section or within 2 tiles of the exit.
TuningA pity counter: +1% per quiet tile, reset on a fight, hard cap of 2 random fights per section. Expect about 1 per section on average. Route danger and level still pick the creature (pick_creature).
FeelThe walk stops on the tile, a rustle puff and the existing ! bang show, then the usual fight card opens. A cheap "sneak past" with a perk is possible later.
Code touch pointstrail_map_make() stops placing E tokens (random maps keep only F); trail_token_event('E') becomes boss-only; trail_go() rolls per path tile; trail_roll_event() (the between-section event) keeps its non-fight outcomes so a section does not double up on fights. I have not changed any game code.

Updated map files: claude/maplewold-trail-maps/maplewold_trail_maps.json and maplewold_trail_maps_22wide.json, re-validated (all other rules unchanged: reachable exit, matching entry rows, finds off the main line).
