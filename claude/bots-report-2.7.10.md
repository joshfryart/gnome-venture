## Human playtest script (15 minutes, `pfight_v1` on for you)

1. New gnome (or reset): fight 1 is the Garden Snail. Do the guided turns. Note any turn you did not know what to do.
2. Fights 2-3 (learner rules): do you ever lose? Is the "costly cell" warning clear?
3. Fight 4: the "New today" card (creature tags, full strength). Read it, then fight. Did a tag surprise you?
4. Level 5+: the Thornburst card. Place a piece next to one on purpose; did the result match the aim number?
5. Fight a creature 4 levels above you, then 4 below. Is the first hard but fair, the second quick?
6. Use Stagger (a creature with it), Pluck and a Luck redraw once each.
7. Pull a bad hand on purpose (pass twice): does the stall nudge and the recap help?
8. End each fight: write one line, "felt too easy / right / too hard", and the creature's name.
