Weather pieces: place, fire

Example for four kinds (sun, rain, wind, mud). The weather piece arrives (about 0.6 s: raise, click, settle), then fires (about 1.2 s) while the resolve numbers play on top. "Single" = the weather piece beside one player piece; "Chain" = the weather piece firing as the second link of a two-piece chain (mud cannot be linked: it acts next to the piece that fires). Real v48 tile art; the resolve rings and numbers are stand-ins.
