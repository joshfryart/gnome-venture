Small creature piece animations (3x3 board)

67 small creatures. Pick one: its pieces are put down one after another (raise, click, wiggle, sound lines), then played as a chain along the OUTER edge of the chain on a 3x3 board. Creature-heavy variant: harder slam, bigger wobble, duller colours. Stand-in pieces; the gold ring and number is the resolve stand-in. Themed look (default): every creature has its own palette, motif (glyph + motion + extra), place personality and sound lines (claude/pf-anim/themes.json); Generic shows the 16 shared families for comparison.
