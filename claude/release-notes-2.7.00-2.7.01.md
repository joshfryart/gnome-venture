## 2.7.00

### Roads are not all the same any more
- **Ferries.** Some crossings are by boat: 20 Petals a crossing.
- **Tunnels.** You need a good enough lantern to go through.
- **Passes.** You need a good enough walking staff to climb them.
- **Toll roads.** Pay 10 Petals for a safer, quicker road, or take the old road for free.
- **Overgrown roads.** Slow going. An axe clears them.
- **Flooded roads.** Slow going. Flood Boots in your satchel halve the slowdown.
- Route cards and the Almanac tell you what kind of road you are on.

### Camp spots
- Every Trail route has one camp spot.
- **Sleep** and **Nap** there are free and safe.
- **Wait** 10 to 60 minutes: you get 1 energy for every 2 minutes. A creature may find you while you wait (half as likely on a paid toll road).

### Sights give better rewards
- Sights now check the conditions they talk about.
- Rewards grow with the level of the region you are in.
- Some Sights give you a discount at a shop.

### Ruins look like ruins
- Nine places now show their own ruined picture while they are ruined.

## New puzzle fights (testing; 2.7.00 and 2.7.01)

### How a chain fires (2.7.00)
- A chain now fires in a fixed order: environment pieces first, then Guard, then set-up pieces, then Hurt and Charm, then Heal.
- The creature no longer gets a free piece just before your turn ends.
- A fight you had already started keeps its old rules.

### Wearables and the Tailor (2.7.00)
- **Six wearables:** Flood Boots, Wayfarer's Hat, Work Gloves, Travel Cloak, Trail Pack, Yarn Scarf.
- Each one is also **one puzzle piece**. Switch it on in a Keepsake slot of your Puzzle box.
- Most come in **two cuts**: two different pieces on the same item. You pick the cut when the item is made.
- **Three ranks:** Common, Lined, Fine. A better rank makes the piece stronger.
- **Where to get them:** the Tailor makes the Boots, Gloves and Cloak. Twigg's sells the Hat (level 5) and the Pack (level 15). The Scarf comes from Cat Fish.
- **The Tailor** is a new garden building (garden level 6, 1,800 Petals). It has 3 levels.
  - Lining a wearable (Common to Lined, Lined to Fine) works 30% of the time, plus 8% for every Tailor level you own across your gardens (up to 78%).
  - A lining that fails gives you a Tailor's Thread. 5 Threads make the next lining a sure thing.
  - Changing the cut costs 1 Thread, or nothing with a level 2 Tailor.
  - A level 3 Tailor makes your first craft each game day for free.
  - A Fine wearable counts as a Rare piece. You can wear it as Lined if your deck already has 3 Rare pieces.
- Your gnome wears what you wear: it shows on your gnome's picture.
- The Almanac has a new page for wearables.

### Gentler first fights (2.7.01)
- Your first 3 fights with the new puzzle rules are gentler:
  - no Thornburst in the environment,
  - creature pieces carry no tags (no Quick, Poison and the like),
  - creature pieces hit a little softer.
- Your 4th fight starts with a short **"New today"** card that says what has changed.

### Clearer warnings (2.7.01)
- **A spot that would hurt you is easy to see.** When you pick a piece, a lit spot whose chain costs you health has a red edge. With a mouse, hover it to see why, for example: "Costs you 4 health: it fires the Garden Snail's Snap."
- **Place the piece there and the line above End turn says it:** "This fires the Garden Snail's Snap. You lose 4 health." Undo takes it back.
- **In your first 5 fights, End turn asks once** before a move that costs health when a safe spot was open. Tap End turn again to play it anyway.

### Better help sheet (2.7.01)
- The "?" sheet now says how a fight is won and lost, what the numbers on a lit spot mean, and what a combo is.
- Each rule on the sheet has its own small picture.

### Wearables on the Trail (2.7.01)
- Five Sights can now give you a Common wearable now and then:
  - the scarecrow: Wayfarer's Hat,
  - the mudslide: Flood Boots,
  - the icicle curtain: Travel Cloak,
  - the woodcutter's stump: Work Gloves,
  - the mossy stair: Trail Pack.

### Fixes (2.7.01)
- The Bramble piece's text now says what it really does: in your chain it fills the creature's Yield bar by a tenth; in the creature's chain you lose a tenth of your health.
- The level 3 Tailor's text now says it is the first **craft** each day that is free (lining never was).
