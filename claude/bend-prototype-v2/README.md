# bend-prototype-v2: all 246 routes with landscape variety

`python3 tools/gen_bendy_trail_proto.py --all --variety` (same roads, joins and rules as `claude/bend-prototype/`, which is unchanged). Added by `--variety`:
- **Stamps by land** (`tools/bend_stamps.py`, shared with `tools/build_maplewold_bendy.py`): 2 to 3 per section, 4+ tiles off the road: grassland (grove, orchard, hedged field, drift, marsh), forest (log, clearing, grove, marsh), coast (dune, rock stack, tide pool), tundra (ice lake, pines, drift), desert (dune, rock stack, drift), mountain (rock stack, pines, drift), wetland (marsh, grove, tide pool), rainforest (grove, log, clearing, marsh), island (dune, tide pool, grove, rock stack).
- **Ponds from the region picture** (`tools/bend_water.py`): blue water blobs of `game/assets/img/scenes/map-<CODE>.png`, filtered by shape, put into the section nearest to them (within 14 region units), on the side they lie.
- Lint: `python3 tools/lint_trail_bends.py claude/bend-prototype-v2/route-*.json` -> 0 problems for all 246.

- **Streams** (`tools/bend_water.py`, `waterways.json`): where the route's curve crosses a stream of its region, the section gets a stream through the road tile along the stream's direction (same code as the Maplewold builder: `stream_ops`), with a ford and a shallow. Maplewold (`NA`) uses the hand-derived network (`tools/maplewold_waterways.py`). Other regions: drawn rivers are read from the picture (teal line components, long and thin; found for SA, SE, EA only, partly), ponds get an outlet to the nearest bigger body, the downstream end of a river is the end nearer to a bigger body or the map edge. Regions whose ground is water-coloured (the Coralscatter) or whose water is not blue (NWE, EE, deserts) get ponds at most.
- **Result:** 16 Maplewold sections have streams; 10 sections in all have ponds (NA 2, CB 4, SA 3, ESA 1); outside Maplewold only 1 section (route 340, CB) has a stream, because the drawn rivers of SA, SE and EA do not cross any of the routes (they are short stop-to-place trails) and most other regions have no detectable water.

Not done here: gates and extra mini-bosses (Maplewold only, by hand). The 16 Maplewold routes in this folder are generated, not the hand-tuned ones in `claude/maplewold-bendy-maps/`.
