**Vision.** The roads of the Hollow went dark because keeping them became nobody's job. The gnome walks out from the Maplewold, makes rest stops their own, replants each area's lost crop, helps the folk who were cut off, and Wren binds what they did into a new Almanac, one area Seal at a time. ## 1. THE CLOCK

### 1.1 What a game day is (code truth)
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| **A game day is the gnome's own day.** `users.game_day` goes up by 1 on each sleep (`game_sleep`); the gnome wakes at 06:00 (`GAME_WAKE_MINUTE` 360). It does **not** turn at midnight | `game_clock.php:123` |
| The gnome's date = season epoch + `game_day` = `gclock_my_date()`. It can run ahead of or behind the real date | `game_clock.php:46, 710` |
| Naps (120 min, +40% energy) and staying up past 24:00 do not change the day (`game_minute` may pass 1440) | `game_clock.php:141`, header |
| Awake day 06:00 to 22:00 = 960 game minutes; after 22:00 actions cost +10% energy per hour. Costs: stop act 15, trail section 10, wander 20, lair 120, repair work 480 | `GCLOCK_TIME_DEFAULTS`, `:611` |
| Setting `game_clock_on` (default 1). **Off = fallback to the real Hollow day**: America/Halifax (`game_timezone`), turning at local midnight | `gclock_my_*`, `hollow_today()`, PRODUCT.md |
| Shared systems stay on the real Hollow clock: market, orders, job board, contests, fair, Cat Fish weeks, visits | `docs/game-time-audit.md` |
| No cron. Decay, happenings, growth and top-ups are computed when read | `stop_living.php:20`, `stops.php:468`, `puzzle_pals.php:864` |

So "per game day" below always means **per sleep of that gnome** (clock on) or per Halifax day (clock off). `docs/game-time-audit.md` (2.6.36) is partly stale: chat (`friends.php:137`) and the adopter's welcome (`stop_adopt.php:334`) already use `gclock_my_date()`.

### 1.2 Rules the whole design obeys
1. Every "once a day", "first each day", "one beat a day" in any proposal = once per game day, keyed on `gclock_my_date()`. Never on `time()`, `now()` or `hollow_today()`.
2. Nothing is timed. No step, errand, escort or thread expires. A gnome who never sleeps loses nothing.
3. Nothing decays that has been earned: steps latch, Standing rows are high-water marks, Seals and marks are for good. Unfilled bars do not decay either (`mark_decay` 0).
4. State is computed on read, from stored day stamps. No job may need a cron.
5. UI text says "after you sleep", not "tomorrow" (audit, point 3).
6. Sleeping more than once in a real day is allowed and is the intended way to play faster. So no proposal reward may rely on a real-day limit, and anything that pays Petals per game day must stay small (the daily-charge perks are capped by design, section 7.4).

## 5. STORY

**Premise.** Under every road runs the Waythread, one old fungus that glows where a lamp is lit and folk pass. The Roundwalkers walked the ring of roads each year, lit lamps, mended, carried post and seed, and wrote the Almanac. A lifetime ago the Round thinned out: each walker thought someone else had the next stretch. That is the Long Quiet. No war, no curse, no villain. The cure, in all 14 areas, is someone making a stop their own. "The Almanac complete" = 14 bound sections plus the page Wren cannot write: the Roll of keepers, signed by the gnome. Mystery: the old Almanac's last page (the old Roll) was cut out.

**Wren.** Naturalist and bookbinder, never gives orders. Walks one area behind with a handcart and only on a lit road; she arrives at the Hearth stop when the Seal is earned. Letters per area, all by event, never timed: cover note (arrival), first-lamp note (first tier 1), leaf notes (zone chapters), Seal letter with four fixed parts (thanks naming a real thing, one correction to the old book, one Roll clue, which doors are open). ## 6. QUEST LINE

### 7.1 Bars
Three bars per stop per gnome: **Repairs, Larder, Cheer**, 0 to 100. A bar that reaches 100 **latches**: it stays full and its perk is on for good ("lasting mark"; stamps Mended / Stocked / Merry). No expiry, no limit on how many, no decay (also none for unfilled bars). Acts at a full bar still pay as normal and add 1 to a "kept up" count (flavour).

### 7.2 Acts feed bars
Every one of the 860 acts feeds one bar; 36% also feed a second at half points. First match wins:
0. An override row in `stop_act_state.txt` (186 today; 60 more suggested).
1. Word tags (name before blurb; whole-word match): fix, craft = Repairs; cook, gather, tend = Larder; trade, play, lore, rest = Cheer.
2. Act kind: craft = Larder if it makes food, else Repairs; lore, gossip = Cheer; buy, snack, swap, seedswap = Larder; contest, pick, power, collect = Cheer; thread = the type's signature bar; the rest = the type's lean.
3. Stop type moves for the game kinds (pick, power, collect).
4. Second bar: buy and swap kinds add Cheer; a moved act adds the bar it came from; else the next tag's bar; never for lore, gossip, thread.

The four answer acts (`mend_board`, `tidy_yard`, `fill_shelf`, `hang_lanterns`) show while their bar is not full, so every bar has a feeder every game day.

### 7.3 Points per game day
`pts = max(1, floor(B x D x W x F)) + V`
| Part | Value |
|---|---|
| B energy cost | 0 = 6, 1 = 9, 2 = 12, 3+ = 15 |
| D nth act today at this stop with this first bar | 1st x1, 2nd x0.5, 3rd and later = 1 point |
| W | win or no dice 1, lose 0.5 |
| F | festival day x2, answer act x1.5 |
| V variety | +2 the first time this act kind is done at this stop today |
| Full round | first time all three bars are fed at a stop in a day: +5 to the lowest unfilled bar |
| Second bar | floor(pts / 2), at least 1; does not count for D |

"Today" = the gnome's game day; D and V are read from `stop_log` (act, kind, day). Sample workshop day: R 20, L 13, C 16. Planning pace: **6 to 10 visit-days per bar.** Sleeping resets D; a nap does not.

### 7.7 Game-day mechanics of perks
- A daily charge is one row `(user_id, day, perk_key)` in `stop_mark_use`, `day` = `gclock_my_date()`. It is taken on use, never banked, and comes back on the next sleep.
- "First visit each day", "first watering each day", "at dawn", "at night", "on windy days", "while it snows" all read the gnome's own clock and weather (`gclock_my_hour`, `gclock_my_weather`).
- `filled_on` is the gnome's game date. Nothing reads it for an expiry.
- Old 7-day perks end on their own game date (`until`); a gnome who does not sleep keeps one until the release that removes the old code. They are never turned into marks.
