Cat Fish room mockups

Stitched Toy style. The room is a CSS/SVG placeholder showing layout, depth and motion; cats are the real flat sprites. Everything below is live: tap the floor.

1. The room (placeholder art, two looks)

Tap the floor: the lure flies there (smaller when farther), the grey cat runs out (shrinks going away, grows coming back), pays Kibble (far catches x1.25), then trots home. Tap the dangling plush fish on the rod (top right) for a rod play: the cat pounces up in place and pays x1.5 (pink pop). The rod flicks toward every floor tap; the fish stay on it. Floor catches pay base, far catches x1.25. Tap the yarn or mouse for a flip. Orange and cream wander and sometimes boop. Up to 3 throws can queue (pips).
